## Vocabulary (5 items)
- **drastically** (adverb)
  - to a very large and sudden degree
  - 大幅度地；急剧地
  - Unfortunately, Apple just drastically increased its prices because of the ongoing memory shortage.
  - 记：dra- 有“猛拉”感 → 价格被猛地拉高

- **entry point** (noun phrase)
  - the lowest-cost or first step into a product line
  - 入门级选择；切入点
  - Up until the launch of MacBook Neo, the MacBook Air represented the entry point to Apple’s laptop lineup.
  - 记：entry = 入口，point = 起点 → 产品线的第一站

- **value proposition** (noun phrase)
  - the main reason a product is worth buying versus alternatives
  - 价值主张；性价比卖点
  - Despite MacBook Neo making its value proposition slightly questionable, I’d still say MacBook Air is a great laptop.
  - 记：你“主张”(proposition) 的“价值”(value) 是什么

- **aforementioned** (adjective)
  - mentioned earlier in the same text
  - 前面提到的
  - It has some compromises compared to the aforementioned MacBook Air, but it’s still incredibly good for the price.
  - 记：afore- = before，mentioned = 提及 → 上文已说的

- **caveat** (noun)
  - a warning or limiting condition you should notice before deciding
  - 注意事项；附带条件
  - You get the best of the best, though there is one major caveat.
  - 记：买高端货前先听一句 “但是……”

## Long Sentences (2-3 items)
- **Original:** Macs aren’t as appealing as they were a few weeks ago, that said, there’s still some decent deals out there – and if you’re in need of a new computer, I’d still say todays Macs are a good value against the rest of the computing market.
  - Structure: concession (aren’t as appealing) + discourse marker (that said) + contrast claim (still decent deals) + dash add-on with conditional (if you’re in need…) + speaker judgment (I’d still say…)
  - Chinese: Mac 不像几周前那么吸引人了；不过话说回来，市场上仍有一些不错的优惠——如果你确实需要一台新电脑，我认为今天的 Mac 相对整个电脑市场仍然划算。
  - Imitation: Our internal tools aren’t as fast as they were last quarter; that said, there are still a few solid upgrades left—and if you need better reporting this month, I’d still say the current stack is a good value against rebuilding from scratch.

- **Original:** Despite MacBook Neo making its value proposition slightly questionable, I’d still say MacBook Air is a great laptop if you want a better experience without needing anything premium.
  - Structure: Despite + gerund clause (concession) + main clause (I’d still say…) + if-clause (condition for the recommendation)
  - Chinese: 尽管 MacBook Neo 让 Air 的价值主张显得有些站不住脚，但如果想要更好体验又不需要顶级配置，我仍认为 MacBook Air 是一台出色的笔记本。
  - Imitation: Despite the cheaper plan making our premium tier slightly questionable, I’d still say the Pro plan is worth it if you want better support without needing enterprise features.

- **Original:** That redesign will only debut with the M5 Pro and M5 Max chip, though – so if you’re someone buying the base model with the M5 chip, you’re still in the clear to buy a new MacBook today.
  - Structure: limiting claim (will only debut with…) + though (soft contrast) + dash result (so…) + conditional (if you’re buying…) + idiom conclusion (in the clear)
  - Chinese: 不过，那次改版只会随 M5 Pro 和 M5 Max 芯片首发——所以如果你买的是搭载基础 M5 芯片的机型，现在入手新 MacBook 仍然没问题。
  - Imitation: That policy change will only affect contractors next quarter, though – so if you’re a full-time employee on the current plan, you’re still in the clear to renew today.

## Useful Chunks (1-2 items)
- **that said** — soft pivot after a negative point; keeps the tone balanced before you offer a brighter side.
- **in the clear** — means “safe / free from risk or blame”; useful when advising someone that a choice won’t create later regret.
