## Vocabulary (5 items)
- **fall behind on** (phrasal verb)
  - fail to keep up with required payments or duties on schedule
  - 拖欠；落后于（付款/进度）
  - Code suggests Apple could restrict leased devices when customers fall behind on payments.
  - 记忆：behind = 落在后面 → 钱没跟上就“落在后面”

- **in good standing** (adjective phrase)
  - compliant with the terms of a contract; not in default
  - （合同）状态良好；未违约
  - If the contract is no longer in good standing, Apple can place the iPhone in Restricted Mode.
  - 记忆：standing = 地位/状态；good standing = “信誉站得住”

- **allowlist** (noun)
  - a fixed list of apps or items that remain permitted
  - 白名单
  - The fixed allowlist in the iOS 27 beta includes Phone, Settings, and Wallet.
  - 记忆：allow + list = 允许清单；比 blacklist 更正面的说法

- **trigger** (verb)
  - cause a process or restriction to start automatically
  - 触发；引发
  - There isn’t a fixed number of missed payments that automatically triggers the restrictions.
  - 记忆：像扣扳机（trigger）一样“一碰就启动”

- **unequivocally** (adverb)
  - in a way that leaves no doubt; clearly and firmly
  - 明确地；毫不含糊地
  - The references described above are unequivocally present in the current code.
  - 记忆：un-（不）+ equivocal（模棱两可）→ 毫不含糊
