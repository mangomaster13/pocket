### 英文改写 Readable rewrite
iOS 27 is set to launch this fall as Apple’s next major iPhone software update, and 9to5Mac highlights three features that could quickly become among the most used. The first is Siri AI. Whatever users thought of Siri in the past, the new version is described as a deep rebuild rather than a light refresh. Apple says it tore Siri down and rebuilt it from the ground up. Users can now ask Siri about almost anything, much as they would ask Google or ChatGPT, and receive informed, helpful answers. Siri can also take more actions on the iPhone, work more consistently across devices, and keep conversations in a dedicated Siri app. After nearly two months with the beta, the writer says the new assistant became a daily go-to almost immediately.

The second feature is automatic proofreading that goes beyond basic spellcheck. The iPhone has long underlined misspelled words, but iOS 27 expands the system in two ways: spellcheck is now powered by Apple Intelligence, and there is a new tool for checking grammar. Because AI can judge context, the phone may suggest a replacement even when a word is spelled correctly but seems used in the wrong way. Misspellings still appear with a red underline, while suggested replacements and grammar issues are marked in blue. That visual difference makes the new suggestions feel optional rather than urgent warnings. Users who dislike the feature can turn proofreading off in Settings, though the writer expects many people will welcome the change.

The third major change is a search overhaul in Spotlight, Photos, and Mail. Spotlight has been rebuilt with a more comprehensive index of on-device content that stays updated throughout the day, and Siri is fully integrated into the search experience. Apple says the same new search foundation also powers Photos and Mail, so finding messages, images, and other items should feel more relevant. In the writer’s testing, the improvement is already noticeable. Taken together, these three updates point to an iOS release focused less on novelty and more on tools people may reach for every day.

### 中文全文翻译
iOS 27 将于今秋作为苹果下一代主要 iPhone 系统更新发布，9to5Mac 重点介绍了三项很可能迅速成为高频功能的新特性。第一项是 Siri AI。无论用户过去如何看待 Siri，新版本都被描述为一次深度重建，而不是轻度升级。苹果表示，它把 Siri“拆到地基”并“从零重建”。用户现在几乎可以像问 Google 或 ChatGPT 一样向 Siri 提问，并得到更知情、更有帮助的回答。Siri 也能在 iPhone 上执行更多操作，在各设备间更稳定地协作，还可通过独立的 Siri App 保存对话。作者使用公测近两个月后表示，新助手几乎立刻成了每日必用工具。

第二项是超越基础拼写检查的自动校对。iPhone 长期以来会用下划线标出拼错的词，但 iOS 27 从两方面扩展了这一能力：拼写检查现由 Apple Intelligence 驱动，并新增语法检查工具。由于 AI 能判断语境，即使某个词拼写正确，若用法看起来不对，手机也可能给出替换建议。拼写错误仍用红色下划线标出，而替换建议和语法问题则用蓝色标记。这种视觉差异让新建议更像可选提示，而不是紧迫警告。不喜欢该功能的用户可在“设置”中关闭校对，不过作者预计很多人会欢迎这一改动。

第三项重大变化是 Spotlight、照片和邮件中的搜索大改版。Spotlight 已用更全面、全天持续更新的本机内容索引重建，并且 Siri 已深度集成到搜索体验中。苹果表示，同一套新搜索基础也用于照片和邮件，因此查找信息、图片等内容应更相关。作者实测后认为提升已经很明显。综合来看，这三项更新表明，这次 iOS 更关注用户每天都会用到的实用工具，而非单纯制造新鲜感。
