### 英文改写 Readable rewrite
iOS 27 is already in public beta and is expected to reach all users this fall. Even so, several features that Bloomberg reporter Mark Gurman described before WWDC still have not appeared in the current beta. 9to5Mac notes that these tools might arrive later in iOS 27.0, or they could land in a follow-up release such as iOS 27.1.

One of the biggest unfinished items is broader chatbot choice inside Siri. ChatGPT is already available, but Apple is said to be opening Siri to other outside assistants as well. In the Search or Ask view, users would be able to switch among chatbots. Apple has reportedly tested Claude and Gemini in addition to ChatGPT. The toggle interface already exists in iOS 27, yet ChatGPT remains the only third-party option for now.

Apple’s Health app is also expected to gain new AI features that once belonged to a larger health-coach project internally known as “Mulberry.” That project was scaled back, but related upgrades—including better blood-sugar tracking and camera-based workout monitoring—are still on the roadmap. These Health changes are not expected in the first iOS 27 release and may be announced after WWDC.

Beyond Siri, Apple plans to let third-party AI providers power image and text generation in tools such as Image Playground and Writing Tools. Today those features can use ChatGPT; later, alternatives could be installed through App Store apps. Apple has again tested Claude and Gemini for this work. Separately, Gurman has said the Camera app will become fully customizable, letting users choose control “widgets” along the top of the interface. A Siri mode has already arrived in Camera, but the deeper customization has not.

Finally, iOS 27 is expected to add software built for Apple’s rumored foldable phone, often called iPhone Ultra. Many Apple apps already support landscape mode in iOS 27, which looks like preparation for a wider screen. Gurman has also reported that side-by-side app multitasking will arrive with the foldable device.

尚未落地的重点之一，是在 Siri 中提供更广泛的聊天机器人选择。ChatGPT 已经可用，但据称苹果还计划向其他外部助手开放 Siri。在“搜索”或“询问”界面中，用户将能够在不同聊天机器人之间切换。除 ChatGPT 外，苹果据报已测试 Claude 与 Gemini。切换界面在 iOS 27 中已经存在，但目前第三方选项仍只有 ChatGPT。

苹果健康应用预计也将获得一批新的 AI 功能，它们原本属于一个内部代号为 “Mulberry” 的更大健康教练项目。该项目已被收缩，但相关升级——包括更完善的血糖追踪，以及用设备摄像头监测锻炼——仍在规划中。这些健康功能预计不会随 iOS 27 首发版本上线，也可能在 WWDC 之后再公布。

除 Siri 外，苹果还计划让第三方 AI 服务商为 Image Playground、Writing Tools 等工具中的图像与文本生成功能提供支持。目前这些功能可调用 ChatGPT；之后，替代方案或可通过 App Store 应用安装。苹果同样为此测试过 Claude 与 Gemini。另外，Gurman 称相机应用将实现全面可定制，用户可自行选择界面顶部的控制“小组件”。相机已加入 Siri 模式，但更深层次的自定义尚未出现。

最后，iOS 27 预计还将加入面向苹果传闻中折叠屏手机（常被称为 iPhone Ultra）的软件能力。许多苹果应用已在 iOS 27 中支持横屏，这看起来像是在为更宽屏幕做准备。Gurman 还报道称，并排应用多任务将随这款折叠设备一并到来。

## 单词 Vocabulary
- **public beta**
  - 英文释义: a pre-release version of software offered to ordinary users for testing before the final launch
  - 中文释义: 向普通用户开放的公开测试版
  - 例句: iOS 27 is currently in beta, and launches to all users this fall.
  - 记忆提示: public（公开的）+ beta（测试版）→ 正式发布前的公开试用

- **toggle between**
  - 英文释义: to switch from one option to another, often with a simple control
  - 中文释义: 在不同选项之间切换
  - 例句: In the Search or Ask view, users will be able to toggle between chatbots.

  - 记忆提示: scale（规模）+ back（往回）→ 把计划往回收

  - 中文释义: 可定制的；可按个人喜好调整的
  - 例句: Apple is planning to make the iOS 27 Camera app interface fully customizable.
  - 记忆提示: custom（定制）+ -izable（可……的）→ 可以自己改
