# English Notes · 2026-07-26

## Summary
- 央行在通胀黏性与增长放缓之间谨慎权衡，强调数据依赖，暂缓过早降息。
- Central banks are balancing sticky inflation against slowing growth, stressing data dependence before any premature easing.

## Vocabulary (5 items)
- **sticky inflation** (noun phrase)
  - English gloss: inflation that remains high and hard to bring down
  - Chinese gloss: 黏性通胀；居高不下的通胀
  - Example: Central banks are walking a narrow path between sticky inflation and slowing growth.
  - Memory tip: sticky 像黏住了——物价“粘”着不下来。

- **data dependence** (noun phrase)
  - English gloss: the practice of setting policy according to incoming economic data
  - Chinese gloss: 数据依赖（按数据定政策）
  - Example: Policymakers now emphasise data dependence, arguing that premature easing could reignite price pressures.
  - Memory tip: depend on data——先看数据，再做决定。

- **premature easing** (noun phrase)
  - English gloss: cutting interest rates too early
  - Chinese gloss: 过早宽松／过早降息
  - Example: Premature easing could reignite price pressures.
  - Memory tip: premature = 太早；easing = 放松政策。

- **reignite** (verb)
  - English gloss: to cause something to start again or become stronger
  - Chinese gloss: 重新点燃；再度引发
  - Example: Premature easing could reignite price pressures.
  - Memory tip: re- + ignite = 再点火，像通胀“复燃”。

- **price in** (phrasal verb)
  - English gloss: to reflect an expected future event in current market prices
  - Chinese gloss: （市场）计入／提前消化（预期）
  - Example: Labour-market cooling is giving markets room to price in a gentler policy trajectory later this year.
  - Memory tip: 价格里已经“装进”了预期。

## Long Sentences (2-3 items)
1. **Original:** Central banks are walking a narrow path between sticky inflation and slowing growth.
   - Structure: main clause (*Central banks are walking a narrow path*) + prepositional phrase (*between A and B*).
   - Chinese: 各国央行正走在黏性通胀与增长放缓之间的狭窄道路上。
   - Imitation: Tech firms are walking a narrow path between rapid innovation and tighter regulation.

2. **Original:** In the wake of successive rate hikes, policymakers now emphasise data dependence, arguing that premature easing could reignite price pressures.
   - Structure: introductory phrase (*In the wake of…*) + main clause (*policymakers now emphasise…*) + participial clause (*arguing that…*).
   - Chinese: 在连续加息之后，政策制定者如今强调数据依赖，认为过早宽松可能再度点燃物价压力。
   - Imitation: In the wake of several product delays, the CEO now emphasises quality control, arguing that a rushed launch could damage the brand.

3. **Original:** That said, labour-market cooling has begun to show up in vacancies and wage growth, giving markets room to price in a gentler policy trajectory later this year.
   - Structure: discourse marker (*That said*) + main clause (*labour-market cooling has begun to show up…*) + result participial phrase (*giving markets room to…*).
   - Chinese: 话虽如此，劳动力市场降温已开始体现在空缺岗位和工资增长上，使市场有空间计入今年晚些时候更为温和的政策路径。
   - Imitation: That said, demand has begun to soften in Europe, giving investors room to price in slower earnings growth next quarter.

## Useful Chunks (1-2 items)
- **in the wake of…** — used to introduce what follows an important event; often for consequences after shocks or policy moves.
- **That said,** — soft contrast marker: acknowledge the previous point, then introduce a balancing fact.
