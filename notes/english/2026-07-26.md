# English Notes · 2026-07-26

## Summary
- 各国央行正夹在顽固通胀与放缓增长之间，强调看数据定政策，同时劳动力市场降温让市场预期年内政策路径更温和。
- Central banks are balancing sticky inflation against slowing growth, stressing data dependence while cooler labour markets let investors price in a gentler policy path later this year.

## Vocabulary (5 items)
1. **sticky inflation** (noun phrase)  
   - English gloss: inflation that stays high and is hard to bring down  
   - Chinese gloss: 顽固通胀  
   - Example: Central banks are walking a narrow path between sticky inflation and slowing growth.  
   - Memory tip: sticky＝粘住，通胀“粘”着不走。

2. **in the wake of** (prepositional phrase)  
   - English gloss: following; as a result of something that happened  
   - Chinese gloss: 在……之后；随着……而来  
   - Example: In the wake of successive rate hikes, policymakers now emphasise data dependence.  
   - Memory tip: wake＝船尾浪，事情过后留下的“余波”。

3. **data dependence** (noun phrase)  
   - English gloss: deciding policy based on incoming economic data rather than a fixed plan  
   - Chinese gloss: 依赖数据（相机抉择）  
   - Example: Policymakers now emphasise data dependence, arguing that premature easing could reignite price pressures.  
   - Memory tip: 政策不靠时间表，靠“数据说了算”。

4. **premature easing** (noun phrase)  
   - English gloss: cutting interest rates too early  
   - Chinese gloss: 过早宽松（过早降息）  
   - Example: Premature easing could reignite price pressures.  
   - Memory tip: premature＝ prematurity（早产），政策“降早了”。

5. **price in** (phrasal verb)  
   - English gloss: reflect an expected future event in current market prices  
   - Chinese gloss: （市场）计入／消化（预期）  
   - Example: Labour-market cooling… giving markets room to price in a gentler policy trajectory later this year.  
   - Memory tip: 把预期“标进”现价里。

## Long Sentences (2-3 items)
1. **Original:** Central banks are walking a narrow path between sticky inflation and slowing growth.  
   - Structure: main clause (*Central banks are walking a narrow path*) + prepositional complement (*between A and B*).  
   - Chinese: 各国央行正走在顽固通胀与增长放缓之间的窄路上。  
   - Imitation: Managers are walking a narrow path between cutting costs and keeping talent.

2. **Original:** In the wake of successive rate hikes, policymakers now emphasise data dependence, arguing that premature easing could reignite price pressures.  
   - Structure: adverbial (*In the wake of…*) + main clause (*policymakers now emphasise…*) + participial clause (*arguing that…*) with a noun clause object (*that premature easing could…*).  
   - Chinese: 在连续加息之后，决策者如今强调依赖数据，认为过早宽松可能再度点燃物价压力。  
   - Imitation: In the wake of several product delays, the CEO now emphasises quality control, arguing that premature launches could damage the brand.

3. **Original:** That said, labour-market cooling has begun to show up in vacancies and wage growth, giving markets room to price in a gentler policy trajectory later this year.  
   - Structure: discourse marker (*That said*) + main clause (*cooling has begun to show up…*) + resultative participial clause (*giving markets room to…*).  
   - Chinese: 话虽如此，劳动力市场降温已开始体现在空缺岗位与工资增速上，使市场有空间计入今年晚些时候更温和的政策路径。  
   - Imitation: That said, weaker demand has begun to show up in orders and inventories, giving firms room to plan a gentler hiring path later this year.

## Useful Chunks (1-2 items)
- **that said** — soft contrast; concede the previous point, then add a balancing fact (*A is true. That said, B…*).  
- **give… room to…** — create space or flexibility for someone to act (*giving markets room to price in…*).
