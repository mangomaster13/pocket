# English Notes · 2026-07-26

## Summary
- 央行在黏性通胀与增长放缓之间谨慎权衡，强调依赖数据、不宜过早降息。
- Central banks are balancing sticky inflation against slowing growth while stressing data-dependent policy.

## Vocabulary (5 items)
- **sticky inflation** (noun phrase)
  - English gloss: inflation that remains high and hard to bring down
  - Chinese gloss: 黏性通胀；居高不下的通胀
  - Example: Central banks are walking a narrow path between sticky inflation and slowing growth.
  - Memory tip: sticky = 黏住，通胀“黏”着不降。

- **in the wake of** (prepositional phrase)
  - English gloss: following; as a result of something that happened
  - Chinese gloss: 在……之后；随着……而来
  - Example: In the wake of successive rate hikes, policymakers now emphasise data dependence.
  - Memory tip: wake 是船尾浪，事情过后留下的“余波”。

- **data dependence** (noun phrase)
  - English gloss: a policy stance of deciding based on incoming economic data
  - Chinese gloss: 数据依赖；依数据而定
  - Example: Policymakers now emphasise data dependence, arguing that premature easing could reignite price pressures.
  - Memory tip: depend on data = 看数据再动手。

- **premature easing** (noun phrase)
  - English gloss: cutting interest rates too early
  - Chinese gloss: 过早宽松；过早降息
  - Example: Premature easing could reignite price pressures.
  - Memory tip: premature = 未熟就摘；easing = 放松政策。

- **price in** (phrasal verb)
  - English gloss: to reflect an expected event in current market prices
  - Chinese gloss: （市场）计入；提前定价
  - Example: Labour-market cooling is giving markets room to price in a gentler policy trajectory later this year.
  - Memory tip: price in = 把预期“算进”现价。

## Long Sentences (2-3 items)
1. **Original:** Central banks are walking a narrow path between sticky inflation and slowing growth.
   - Structure: main clause (*Central banks are walking a narrow path*) + prepositional phrase (*between A and B*).
   - Chinese: 各国央行正走在黏性通胀与增长放缓之间的狭窄道路上。
   - Imitation: Startups are walking a narrow path between rapid hiring and tight cash flow.

2. **Original:** In the wake of successive rate hikes, policymakers now emphasise data dependence, arguing that premature easing could reignite price pressures.
   - Structure: adverbial phrase (*In the wake of...*) + main clause (*policymakers now emphasise...*) + participial clause (*arguing that...*).
   - Chinese: 在连续加息之后，政策制定者现在强调依赖数据，认为过早宽松可能再度点燃物价压力。
   - Imitation: In the wake of several product delays, the team now emphasises quality checks, arguing that rushed releases could damage trust.

3. **Original:** That said, labour-market cooling has begun to show up in vacancies and wage growth, giving markets room to price in a gentler policy trajectory later this year.
   - Structure: discourse marker (*That said*) + main clause (*cooling has begun to show up...*) + resultative participial phrase (*giving markets room to...*).
   - Chinese: 话虽如此，劳动力市场降温已开始体现在空缺岗位与薪资增长上，使市场有空间计入今年晚些时候更温和的政策路径。
   - Imitation: That said, demand has begun to soften in Europe, giving investors room to price in slower expansion next quarter.

## Useful Chunks (1-2 items)
- **That said,** — soft contrast marker: concede the previous point, then introduce a counterbalance.
- **walk a narrow path between A and B** — describe a delicate trade-off between two risks or goals.
