## Vocabulary (5 items)
- **escalation** (noun)
  - an increase in intensity or seriousness
  - 升级；加剧
  - The US has imposed new tariffs on 60 trading partners in the latest escalation in the trade war.
  - 记忆：escalate = 往上爬 → 冲突“升级”

- **levy** (noun)
  - a tax or official charge, especially on imports
  - 征税；税款（常指关税）
  - The tariffs replace an identical levy which expires on Friday.
  - 记忆：政府“levy”钱，像从进口货上“抽一层”

- **distortive** (adjective)
  - causing markets or trade to work unfairly or inefficiently
  - 扭曲性的；造成扭曲的
  - Greer called forced labour both a human rights abuse and a distortive trade practice.
  - 记忆：distort = 变形 → 扭曲贸易规则

- **comparative advantage** (noun phrase)
  - a relative edge in cost or competitiveness versus another party
  - 比较优势
  - The BCC said the UK had lost its comparative advantage against the EU.
  - 记忆：不是绝对最强，而是“相对更划算”

- **tit-for-tat** (adjective / noun)
  - retaliation in kind; an equal response to another’s action
  - 以牙还牙的；针锋相对
  - The US and China have been embroiled in a tit-for-tat tariffs war.
  - 记忆：你加一档，我也加一档 → 对打

  - Structure: [Main clause: The US has imposed new tariffs…] + [prepositional phrase: on 60 trading partners] + [adverbial: in the latest escalation…] + [reduced relative: reignited by…] + [time clause: when he returned…]

- **Original:** William Bain told the BBC the EU has a 10% all-inclusive deal for tariffs on its goods, while the UK is facing 10% universal tariffs on top of any duties imposed on individual goods.
  - Structure: [Reporting: William Bain told the BBC] + [clause A: the EU has…] + [contrast marker: while] + [clause B: the UK is facing…] + [additive phrase: on top of any duties…]

  - Imitation: Our counsel told the team the US client has a flat 10% service fee, while the UK client is facing 10% plus any local compliance charges.

## Useful Chunks (1-2 items)
- **on top of** — adds an extra burden beyond what already exists; useful in cost, tax, and workload talk (*10% tariffs on top of any duties*).
- **like for like** — means an equivalent swap with little real change; handy in pricing, contracts, and policy updates (*a "like for like" swap with the regime that expired*).
