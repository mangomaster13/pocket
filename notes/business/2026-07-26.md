## Vocabulary (5 items)
- **plummet** (verb)
  - English gloss: to fall or drop suddenly and steeply
  - Chinese gloss: 骤降、大幅下跌
  - Example: Rightmove searches for rental properties that allow pets have plummeted since law changes gave renters more rights.
  - Memory tip: 想象股价“垂直坠下”（plumb/plummet 都有“垂直”感）

- **unreasonably refuse** (verb phrase)
  - English gloss: to reject something without a fair or valid reason
  - Chinese gloss: 无理拒绝
  - Example: Landlords in England cannot unreasonably refuse pets in their properties under changes made in May.
  - Memory tip: un-（不）+ reason（理由）→ 没正当理由就拒绝

- **balance of power** (noun phrase)
  - English gloss: the relative strength or advantage between two sides
  - Chinese gloss: 力量对比、权势天平
  - Example: Tenants now understand the balance of power has shifted.
  - Memory tip: 天平（balance）两边的权力（power）谁更重

- **blanket 'no pets' policy** (noun phrase)
  - English gloss: a one-size-fits-all rule that bans pets in all cases
  - Chinese gloss: 一刀切的“禁止养宠物”政策
  - Example: Landlords can no longer rely on a blanket 'no pets' policy to deter applications.
  - Memory tip: blanket 像毯子盖住一切 → “全面、一刀切”

- **levy** (verb)
  - English gloss: to impose or charge a fee, tax, or surcharge
  - Chinese gloss: 征收（费用/附加费）
  - Example: Landlords should not be levying pet surcharges as this fell outside permitted fees.
  - Memory tip: levy ≈ “加收一笔钱”；商务邮件里常见 levy a fee/tax
