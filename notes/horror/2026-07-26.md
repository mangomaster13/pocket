## Vocabulary (5 items)
- **make no secret of** (idiom)
  - English gloss: to openly show or admit something without hiding it
  - Chinese gloss: 毫不隐瞒；公开表示
  - Example: Ryan Gosling has made no secret of the fact that he’d love to play Ghost Rider.
  - Memory tip: secret 被“make no”否定 → 并不保密

- **iconic** (adjective)
  - English gloss: widely recognized as a classic symbol of something
  - Chinese gloss: 标志性的；极具代表性的
  - Example: Ryan Gosling will play the iconic Marvel character in a Ghost Rider movie.
  - Memory tip: icon（偶像/图标）+ -ic → 像偶像一样经典

- **in the wake of** (prepositional phrase)
  - English gloss: after and as a result of something
  - Chinese gloss: 在……之后；紧随……而来
  - Example: Luna was set to star in his own Ghost Rider spinoff series in the wake of that small screen appearance.
  - Memory tip: wake 是船尾浪迹 → 事情过后留下的“余波”

- **squash** (verb)
  - English gloss: to stop or cancel a plan forcefully before it succeeds
  - Chinese gloss: 压垮；取消；扼杀
  - Example: That Hulu project ended up being squashed before it ever had a chance.
  - Memory tip: 想象计划像水果被“压扁”了

- **bring ... back to life** (phrasal verb)
  - English gloss: to revive or restore something that had faded or ended
  - Chinese gloss: 让……重获新生；重新激活
  - Example: Ryan Gosling making it clear that he’d love to bring the character back to life.
  - Memory tip: back to life = 回到生命状态 → 复活角色

## Long Sentences (2-3 items)
- **Original:** Ryan Gosling (Project Hail Mary) has made no secret of the fact that he’d love to play Ghost Rider in a Marvel Cinematic Universe movie, and it’s now officially happening.
  - Structure: [Subject + idiom] + [appositive] + [of the fact that + clause] + [and + result clause]
  - Chinese: 瑞恩·高斯林（《冰雹玛丽计划》）毫不掩饰自己很想在漫威电影宇宙中出演恶灵骑士，而这件事现在正式成真了。
  - Imitation: She has made no secret of the fact that she wants a leadership role, and the promotion is now officially happening.

- **Original:** Luna was set to star in his own Ghost Rider spinoff series in the wake of that small screen appearance, but that Hulu project ended up being squashed before it ever had a chance.
  - Structure: [Subject + was set to + verb] + [in the wake of + noun] + [but + contrast clause with ended up being + past participle]
  - Chinese: 卢纳原本要在那次电视亮相之后主演他自己的《恶灵骑士》衍生剧，但那个 Hulu 项目还没来得及起步就被砍掉了。
  - Imitation: The writer was set to launch her own podcast in the wake of the bestseller, but the deal ended up being squashed before it ever had a chance.

- **Original:** Fans have been waiting for Ghost Rider to join the Marvel Cinematic Universe for many years now, with Ryan Gosling making it clear that he’d love to bring the character back to life.
  - Structure: [Present perfect continuous main clause] + [with + subject + V-ing + that-clause]
  - Chinese: 粉丝们已经等了好多年，盼着恶灵骑士加入漫威电影宇宙，而瑞恩·高斯林也明确表示很想让这个角色重获新生。
  - Imitation: Readers have been waiting for the sequel for many years now, with the author making it clear that she’d love to bring the story back to life.
