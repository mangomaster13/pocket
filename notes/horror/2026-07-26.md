## Vocabulary (5 items)
- **raise eyebrows** (idiom / verb phrase)
  - English gloss: to surprise or mildly shock people; to cause curiosity or disapproval
  - Chinese gloss: 令人侧目；引起惊讶或非议
  - Example: The trailer for Ti West’s upcoming adaptation raised a lot of eyebrows when it dropped earlier this week.
  - Memory tip: 眉毛一扬 = “咦？”——有人觉得意外或不妥。

- **derivative** (adjective)
  - English gloss: unoriginal; too clearly copied from something else
  - Chinese gloss: 缺乏新意的；衍生拼凑的
  - Example: Plenty of folks initially dismissed Ebenezer as a derivative project.
  - Memory tip: 词根 derive（源自）→ 太像“抄来的”。

- **cash in on** (phrasal verb)
  - English gloss: to exploit a trend or situation for profit
  - Chinese gloss: 趁机捞一笔；蹭热度变现
  - Example: It was dismissed as a project meant to cash in on the recent wave of unnecessary prequels.
  - Memory tip: cash（现金）+ in on（针对某事）= 拿热门话题换钱。

- **helm** (verb)
  - English gloss: to direct or be in charge of (a film, project, etc.)
  - Chinese gloss: 执导；掌舵
  - Example: The movie is being helmed by a genuine horror auteur.
  - Memory tip: helm 本义是船舵 → 掌舵拍片。

- **instrumental** (adjective)
  - English gloss: playing an important role in making something happen
  - Chinese gloss: 起关键作用的
  - Example: Leech was instrumental in crafting the iconic look of history’s most influential phantoms.
  - Memory tip: instrument（工具）→ 某人就像“关键工具/关键推手”。

## Long Sentences (2-3 items)
- **Original:** While plenty of folks initially dismissed Ebenezer as a derivative project meant to cash in on the recent wave of unnecessary prequels that expand the backstory of beloved characters (such as 2023’s Wonka), Charles Dickens’ original novel already made a point of exploring Ebenezer Scrooge’s origins in detail – meaning that this new film is likely just a particularly faithful adaptation of the source material under a different name.
  - Structure: While [concession clause] + main clause (Dickens… in detail) – meaning that [result/interpretation clause]
  - Chinese: 虽然不少人起初把它斥为蹭“前传潮”的衍生之作（比如 2023 的《旺卡》），但狄更斯原著本就细致写过斯克鲁奇的身世——因此这部新片更可能只是换了个名字、却相当忠实的原著改编。
  - Imitation: While many viewers first wrote the reboot off as a derivative attempt to cash in on nostalgia, the novel had already mapped the hero’s past in detail – meaning the film may simply be a faithful retelling under a new title.

- **Original:** That being said, one detail that immediately jumped out at me during the brief teaser (other than a blink-and-you’ll-miss-it nod to Johnny Depp’s iconic bedroom death scene from the original A Nightmare on Elm Street) is the fact that many of Ebenezer’s ghosts appear to have been directly lifted from the iconic John Leech illustrations that accompanied the first edition of Dickens’ novel.
  - Structure: Discourse marker + [one detail that… ] is the fact that [content clause]; parenthesis adds a secondary observation
  - Chinese: 话虽如此，短预告里立刻抓住我眼球的一点（除了对《猛鬼街》约翰尼·德普卧室死状那一闪而过的致敬）是：许多鬼魂造型似乎直接取自伴随狄更斯小说初版的约翰·利奇经典插画。
  - Imitation: That being said, one detail that jumped out during the short clip (other than a blink-and-you’ll-miss-it costume gag) is the fact that the creature design looks lifted from nineteenth-century engravings.

- **Original:** And since the novel is commonly said to have popularized spiritualism in Victorian England, much of the ghostly iconography that we still see in genre media to this very day (including some of Ti West’s previous films) can be traced back to the author’s partnership with Leech.
  - Structure: And since [cause] , [result: much of X … can be traced back to Y]; parenthesis gives examples
  - Chinese: 而既然这部小说常被认为推动了维多利亚时期英格兰招魂术/唯灵论的流行，那么我们至今仍在类型作品里看到的许多鬼魂意象（包括 Ti West 此前一些影片），都可以追溯到作者与利奇的合作。
