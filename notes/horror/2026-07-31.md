### 英文改写 Readable rewrite
Killer shark movies are everywhere this year—more in number than in quality—and Jesse V. Johnson’s *Above & Below* is the latest entry. Johnson, known for action films, steps into animal-attack horror for the first time. The film delivers hungry sharks and a high body count, yet most of the killing comes from dangerous men rather than from the creatures in the water.

A group of friends on vacation in Mexico accepts an exclusive cage-diving trip on a luxury catamaran after Kyle, the group’s screw-up, brings them an offer they cannot refuse. Kyle’s sister Tatiana is hesitant, but her boyfriend and their couple friends overrule her. Quiet heavies with matching designer bags soon join the passenger list. Another boat arrives, and three armed men climb aboard—among them a terrifically entertaining Antonio Banderas. Before long, the sharks are no longer hungry, and the real nightmare is underway.

Like recent titles such as *Dangerous Animals* and *The Bay*, the film treats sharks as an underlying threat instead of the only obstacle. The true antagonists are greedy, murderous humans, and the actors playing those villains give the movie its most engaging performances. Writer Anthony Steven Giordano gives the friends little drama or depth: Kyle is a troublemaker, Tatiana plans to break up with TJ, and otherwise there is not much between them. The bad guys, by contrast, have more to play with. Louis Mandylor’s Martin is a villainous loose cannon; Jess Liaudin’s Garrison has a charismatic gentility that contrasts with his muscular frame. Banderas’s Carlos steals the show, shifting from joyous energy to abject cruelty and injecting craziness into otherwise serious, sometimes meandering beats.

The biggest performance issue, the review says, is Laura Marano’s Tatiana—especially in underwater dialogue. The SCUBA helmets are wired so divers can talk, and the scenes are shot underwater and look good, but the later recorded lines never match the intensity of bodies falling and sharks biting. Still, Johnson crafts thrills and suspense, cutting between criminal threats above and shark danger below while keeping tension alive. Viewers keep guessing who will survive. Below the surface, characters trapped in a cage on the ocean floor run low on air and must retrieve tanks from the dead, leading to eerie encounters with chewed-upon corpses. One sequence with Tatiana, Martin, and a great white is a high point of creativity and execution. The sharks are CG and mostly look solid; the CG blood is what most clearly looks fake.

The story is set in Mexico but filmed in Bulgaria and the Canary Islands; limited land scenes and convincing offshore and underwater work sustain the illusion of dangerous isolation. In the end, Johnson’s direction and the turns by Banderas, Mandylor, and Liaudin may make *Above & Below* worth watching. The script teases a theme about the danger of keeping secrets but ultimately does little with it—or with logic. A stronger script could have given everyone more to work with, yet audiences rarely come to these movies for the writing. *Above & Below* swam into select theaters on July 29, 2026.

### 中文全文翻译
今年杀手鲨鱼片铺天盖地——数量多于质量——而杰西·V·约翰逊的《Above & Below》是最新一部。约翰逊以动作片见长，首次涉足动物袭击类恐怖。影片既有饥渴的鲨鱼，也有可观的死亡人数，但大多数杀戮来自危险的人，而非水中的生物。

一群朋友在墨西哥度假时，团队里的麻烦制造者凯尔带来一个无法拒绝的邀约：登上豪华双体动力艇，参加保证能近距离见到鲨鱼的专属笼潜之旅。凯尔的姐姐塔蒂亚娜犹豫不决，却被男友和朋友夫妇否决。随后，拎着配套名牌包的沉默打手登船；另一艘船靠拢，三名武装男子爬上来——其中包括表现极其抢眼的安东尼奥·班德拉斯。不久鲨鱼便不再饥饿，真正的噩梦开始了。

与近期的《Dangerous Animals》《The Bay》类似，本片把鲨鱼当作潜在威胁，而非唯一障碍。真正的反派是贪婪、嗜杀的人类，饰演这些反派的演员贡献了全片最抓人的表演。编剧安东尼·史蒂文·乔达诺几乎没给这群朋友写多少戏剧冲突或人物深度：凯尔是捣蛋鬼，塔蒂亚娜打算跟TJ分手，其余关系单薄。反派反而更有发挥空间。路易斯·曼迪勒的马丁是狂躁失控的恶棍；杰斯·利奥丹的加里森带着一种迷人的温文尔雅，与他结实的身形形成对照。班德拉斯的卡洛斯抢尽风头，能从欢快活力瞬间翻转为彻头彻尾的残忍，给原本严肃、偶尔拖沓的段落注入疯狂乐趣。

评论认为最大的表演问题出在劳拉·马拉诺饰演的塔蒂亚娜——尤其是水下对白。潜水头盔装有通话装置，水下实拍画面好看，但后期录制的台词完全跟不上尸体坠落、鲨鱼撕咬的紧张感。尽管如此，约翰逊仍善于制造融入人物的惊悚与悬念：在甲板上的犯罪威胁与水下的鲨鱼危险之间来回切换，却很少丢掉张力。观众始终猜不透谁能活下来。水下，人物被困在沉到洋底的鲨鱼笼里，氧气将尽，不得不从死者身上取气瓶，从而与被啃咬过的尸体发生诡异互动。塔蒂亚娜、马丁与一条大白鲨的一场戏，是创意与执行的高光。鲨鱼为CG，整体观感不错；最假的是CG血迹。

故事设定在墨西哥，实拍于保加利亚与加那利群岛；陆地戏有限，离岸与水下拍摄足以维持危险孤绝的幻觉。归根结底，约翰逊的调度以及班德拉斯、曼迪勒、利奥丹的表演，或许让《Above & Below》值得一看。剧本暗示“保守秘密的危险后果”这一主题，最终却几乎未展开，逻辑也经不起推敲。更好的剧本本可给他们更多发挥空间，不过观众本就不为剧本而来。影片于2026年7月29日登陆部分影院。
