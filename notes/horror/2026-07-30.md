### 英文改写 Readable rewrite
Among a wave of killer-shark releases in 2026, *The Devil’s Mouth* stands out as the best of the year so far. It is the third new shark film of July, the fifth of the summer, and the sixth of the year—and, according to Bloody Disgusting, it is also the one that actually delivers. The praise is not faint: the review calls it a good, fun, and frequently thrilling entry in a subgenre often filled with weak or merely okay titles.

The story follows five friends in their twenties on a Thailand vacation meant as one last adventure before adult life begins. After a boating mishap leaves Sara, played by Kathryn Newton, stung by jellyfish, the group still proceeds with a guided freshwater cave swim the next day. Their guide assures them the setting should be free of dangerous ocean animals. Sara also wants to see a spot her late father loved. Once inside, they discover storm surges have pushed sea life into the caves. Most creatures die in the fresh water, but one survives and thrives: an angry bull shark that forces the survivors through a tightening, crumbling maze of underwater passages.

Familiar shark-movie ingredients are all present—an attractive cast, an exotic location, a CG creature, and a rising body count—but the film executes them more carefully than many recent peers. Set pieces balance suspense and thrills, and the production keeps visual interest high after leaving Thailand’s sunlit coast for water-filled caves filled with jagged rock, bioluminescent trails, vast bat caverns, and narrow passages. The shark appears in both CG and prosthetics; some late digital work is less convincing, yet many attacks feel harrowing, with victims thrashed and dragged in ways that recall the terror of *Jaws* more than disposable CGI kills.

Newton anchors the cast with charisma and relative level-headedness as Sara rebuilds trust after grief, while Lana Condor’s Max is both loyal friend and frustrating force. The review frames the film as a story of redemption for Sara and Max—and, fittingly, for director Jeff Wadlow, whose earlier horror titles include *Truth or Dare*, *Imaginary*, *Fantasy Island*, and *Cry_Wolf*. *The Devil’s Mouth* is called not only the year’s best shark film but also the strongest of Wadlow’s nine movies, thanks to stronger lighting, design, geography, and kills that grow sillier without losing entertainment value. It arrives on Prime Video as well-crafted survival horror for genre fans who want a cool shark, gripping kills, and a memorably claustrophobic setting.

### 中文全文翻译
在2026年一波杀手鲨鱼片浪潮中，《魔鬼之口》脱颖而出，被评为迄今为止本年度最佳。它是七月上映的第三部新鲨鱼片、今夏第五部、今年第六部——而据 Bloody Disgusting 的说法，它也是真正能交出成绩的那一部。这份褒奖并不敷衍：影评称它是一部好看、有趣、且时常惊险刺激的类型之作，远胜许多平庸或勉强过得去的同类影片。

故事讲述五位二十多岁的朋友在泰国度假，想在真正进入成人生活前再狂欢一场。一次乘船意外让凯瑟琳·牛顿饰演的萨拉被水母蜇伤，但第二天他们仍按计划参加有导游带队的淡水洞穴游泳。导游保证那里不应有危险的海洋生物。萨拉也想亲眼看看已故父亲喜爱的地方。进入洞穴后，他们发现风暴涌浪已把海洋生物冲了进来。多数生物因淡水而死，却有一头愤怒的公牛鲨活了下来并占据食物链顶端，幸存者不得不穿过越来越狭窄、不断崩塌的水下通道求生。

帅哥美女阵容、异域景致、CG怪物与不断上升的死亡人数等鲨鱼片常规元素一应俱全，但本片执行得比近年来许多同类更用心。动作场面兼顾悬疑与刺激，离开泰国阳光海岸进入充水洞穴后，画面依然抓人：嶙峋岩石、生物荧光水痕、巨大蝙蝠洞与逼仄通道交替出现。鲨鱼同时用CG与义肢呈现；后期部分数字效果略弱，但许多攻击场面令人毛骨悚然，受害者被撕咬甩打、拖入深水，比廉价的快速CG击杀更接近《大白鲨》式的恐怖。

牛顿以魅力与相对冷静的头脑撑起表演，萨拉在丧亲之后重新学会信任自己；拉娜·康德饰演的马克斯既是忠诚好友，又令人又爱又恼。影评将影片视为萨拉与马克斯的救赎故事——也恰似导演杰夫·瓦德洛的一次自我救赎，其此前恐怖作品包括《真心话大冒险》《幻象》《梦幻岛》与《狼哭》。影评称《魔鬼之口》不仅是今年最佳鲨鱼片，也是瓦德洛九部作品中最强的一部，光线、美术、空间感与杀戮场面都明显进步，后半虽渐趋荒唐却仍够娱乐。它作为精心打造的生存恐怖片登陆Prime Video，献给想看酷鲨鱼、抓人击杀与令人难忘的幽闭场景的类型片观众。
