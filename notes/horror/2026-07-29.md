### 英文改写 Readable rewrite
Director Takashi Shimizu’s *The Mouths* begins with a clean hook: an enormous, threatening tree at the edge of a cemetery hides a killer curse. Touch it, make a wish, and the horror starts. Fans of Shimizu, best known for the *Ju-on* films, will not be surprised that the story refuses to stay simple. He favors non-linear storytelling and haunting figures, and this film largely follows that familiar path.

Masahiro Yamaura’s screenplay adapts Sesuji’s novella *A Questionnaire About the Mouth*. A questionnaire framing device opens the movie, then nearly vanishes, only to return in the closing moments in a highly amusing way. Between those bookends, four friends chase an urban legend: soft-spoken Shota, sensitive Mirei, outgoing Ann, and Ann’s boastful boyfriend Tatsuya. Early scenes on the drive to the cemetery and at a convenience store already hint at uneasy relationships, while police interviews in the present day interrupt the night’s events with ominous statements from the survivors.

The first act works because the film slowly builds tension around what went wrong. Even after the teens reach the cemetery, Shota insists they approach the tree one by one in a set order, stretching the wait until the dread feels both tantalizing and agonizing. When Ann disappears, the remaining friends search for her and appear to be haunted by both her and the tree. Shimizu’s gift for ghostly figures lurking in the background returns, including a striking image of a hanged woman with a grotesquely stretched neck. Wet mud around the tree slurps at shoes, and cicadas covering the cursed bark add buzz and crunch to the sound design.

The middle stretch can feel too familiar, which makes the last act’s upheaval exciting at first. Many characters prove unreliable narrators, and several earlier interactions were shaped by malicious intent. That revisionist filter is provocative, but the creative team struggles to deliver it without messy confusion. At least one major revelation is muddled by unclear visuals and dialogue, muting its power. Solid turns from the four teens, especially Rihito Itagaki as Shota, help, yet some supporting roles feel superfluous. Overall, *The Mouths* is a decent addition for Shimizu fans—strong scare setups in places, including library and driving scenes—but its clumsy big reveal and weaker stretches leave it slightly inessential. It made its North American premiere at the 2026 Fantasia International Film Festival; release info remains TBD.

### 中文全文翻译
清水崇的新作《The Mouths》开场钩子很干脆：墓地边缘有一棵巨大而危险的树，里面藏着杀人诅咒。摸一下、许个愿，恐怖便开始了。熟悉清水——尤其是《咒怨》系列——的观众不会惊讶故事并不简单：他偏爱非线性叙事与萦绕不去的亡灵，而本片大体沿用这一熟悉路线。

剧本由山浦雅宏执笔，改编自 Sesuji 的中篇小说《关于嘴巴的问卷》。影片用问卷式框架开场，随后几乎被搁置，直到结尾才以十分有趣的方式回归。在此之间，四个朋友追查一个都市传说：沉默寡言的翔太、敏感的美怜、外向的安，以及安爱吹牛的男友龙也。前往墓地途中与便利店的早段戏已暗示关系不安，而“现在时”的警方问话不断打断当晚事件，幸存者们说出不祥的证词。

第一幕之所以奏效，是因为影片围绕“当晚究竟出了什么事”慢慢积蓄张力。即便到了墓地，翔太仍坚持按固定顺序一人一人接近那棵树，把等待拉长，让恐惧既诱人又难熬。安失踪后，其余人四处寻找，并似乎同时被她与那棵树纠缠。清水擅用背景中窥视的幽灵身影，其中包括缢死女子脖子被拉得畸形的惊悚画面。树周湿泥吸吮鞋底，树皮上的蝉鸣与碎壳则构成鲜明的拟音设计。

中段略显熟悉，因此最后一幕颠覆先前认知起初令人兴奋：许多人原来是不可靠叙述者，早前若干互动也被恶意操纵。这种“修正滤镜”富有挑衅性与吸引力，但创作团队难以避免混乱。至少有一处本该极具冲击力的揭示，却因画面与对白含糊而失色。四名少年表演扎实，板垣李光斗饰演的翔太戏路最宽，但部分配角显得多余。总体而言，《The Mouths》对清水粉丝仍是一次尚可的观影——图书馆与侦探驾车等处的惊吓铺陈可圈可点——但笨拙的大揭示与薄弱段落使其略显非必需。影片在 2026 年奇幻国际电影节完成北美首映，上映信息待定。

## 单词 Vocabulary
- **non-linear**
  - 英文释义: arranged out of chronological order; not moving straight from beginning to end
  - 中文释义: 非线性的；不按时间顺序展开的
  - 例句: The Japanese director delights in non-linear narratives and haunting specters.
  - 记忆提示: non-（非）+ linear（直线）→ 叙事不“一条线走到底”

- **urban legend**
  - 英文释义: a widely shared modern story that people treat as true, often spooky or shocking
  - 中文释义: 都市传说
  - 例句: The film tells the story of four friends who investigate an urban legend of a cursed tree.
  - 记忆提示: urban（城市）+ legend（传说）→ 流传在现代城市里的怪谈

- **tantalizing**
  - 英文释义: tempting or exciting because something desired is close but still out of reach
  - 中文释义: 撩人的；令人心痒的（因可望而不可即）
  - 例句: The wait for the action to kick in is both tantalizing and agonizing.
  - 记忆提示: 联想神话里的 Tantalus——永远够不着食物与水

- **unreliable narrator**
  - 英文释义: a storyteller whose account cannot be fully trusted because of bias, lies, or limited knowledge
  - 中文释义: 不可靠叙述者
  - 例句: Most of these people are revealed to be unreliable narrators.
  - 记忆提示: unreliable（不可靠）+ narrator（叙述者）→ 别把他们的说法当真

- **superfluous**
  - 英文释义: unnecessary; more than what is needed
  - 中文释义: 多余的；不必要的
  - 例句: Many of the supporting characters feel a bit superfluous.
  - 记忆提示: super-（过度）+ fluous（流动/溢出）→ 多到溢出来、用不上

  - 仿写: For longtime fans of the series, there’s plenty to appreciate, particularly the way the director sets up mysteries and pays them off, including a tense hospital scene and later when the heroine returns home alone.
