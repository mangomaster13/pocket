# Shannon Lay Reinvents Her Sound on Past the Veil

Source · 2026-07-29 · (no link)

## Lead
Five years after her last album of original songs, Shannon Lay returns with *Past the Veil*, trading sparse folk for lush jazz and trip-hop textures. Pitchfork praises the record’s cohesive voice and memorable melodies, while noting that a few experimental production choices blur the music’s immediacy.

### 英文改写 Readable rewrite
Five years have passed since Shannon Lay last released an album of original songs. In that time, the Los Angeles singer-songwriter sharpened her craft on 2023’s *Covers Vol. 1*, reworking classics such as Elliott Smith’s “Angeles” and the Velvet Underground’s “I’m Set Free” with her smoky, intimate voice. Now, on *Past the Veil*, she reinvents her own sound. Where 2021’s *Geist* favored sparse instrumentation and close-up production, the new record leans into lush, jazzy arrangements that feel alternately vaporous and glitchy—yet still arresting and immediate.

According to Pitchfork’s Linnie Greene, Lay’s voice is what holds this mix of influences together. On the trip-hop opener “Moving,” she pleads, “Bring us back to reality / Bring us back to love,” as quivering strings swell like a James Bond climax and the mix opens into a wide, stereophonic space. Standout track “The Calling” places her against a Carpenters-like piano melody and warm AM-radio harmonies before lifting into a key change. “Whom Gods Destroy” features a sharp, slightly dissonant flute line. The album closes with “I Need More Water,” whose disco pulse and double-claps update a classic groove while Lay questions hustle culture over a Billy Preston–style piano riff: “What if we called it success to sit around in the shade for days?”

Not every risk lands cleanly. On “Room for Less,” stuttering electronics recall Massive Attack but sit uneasily against Lay’s earthy alto. On “Sky Song,” loops and pedal steel can feel like static washing over her voice. Some lyrics, taken alone, sound like familiar affirmations—“You are more than your worst day”—yet Greene argues that Lay’s delivery, and the rising arrangements around her, give those lines real weight. The press materials cite philosopher Alan Watts, and the songs often build steadily skyward over melodies Lay has long mastered.

In the end, *Past the Veil* is framed as music for night drives and quiet, half-dreaming moments—a glimpse past everyday exhaustion. Embracing jazz mysticism and trip-hop rhythms, Lay takes worthwhile risks on an arresting new album.

### 中文全文翻译
距香农·莱上一张原创专辑已过去五年。其间，这位洛杉矶唱作人在2023年的《Covers Vol. 1》上打磨技艺，以烟雾般私密的嗓音重塑了埃利奥特·史密斯的《Angeles》、地下丝绒的《I'm Set Free》等经典。如今在《Past the Veil》上，她重新塑造了自己的声音。相较2021年《Geist》偏爱的稀疏乐器与贴身制作，新专转向丰满、偏爵士的编曲，时而空灵、时而带故障感，却始终抓耳而直接。

Pitchfork乐评人林妮·格林（Linnie Greene）认为，莱的嗓音把种种影响拢成一体。在偏trip-hop的开场曲《Moving》里，她恳求“把我们带回现实／带回爱”，弦乐如007高潮般颤动，声场立体展开。亮点曲《The Calling》以木匠兄妹式钢琴与暖调AM电台和声托起她的歌声，后半段推向转调。《Whom Gods Destroy》有一段锋利、略带不谐和的长笛。专辑以《I Need More Water》收尾：迪斯科节拍与双击掌为经典律动注入现代感，莱在比利·普雷斯顿风格的钢琴riff上质疑“拼命文化”，唱道：“如果我们把整天坐在树荫下也叫作成就，会怎样？”

并非每次冒险都完美。《Room for Less》中结巴的电子打击令人想起Massive Attack，却与莱泥土般的中低音略显冲突；《Sky Song》里的循环与钢棒吉他有时像静电，淹没了歌声。单独听来，部分歌词近乎陈词滥调——如“你远不止最糟糕的那一天”——但格林认为，莱的演绎与层层抬升的编曲赋予这些句子分量。通稿提及哲学家艾伦·瓦茨，而歌曲常在她早已擅长的永恒旋律上稳步升腾。

最终，《Past the Veil》被写成适合夜驾与半梦半醒时刻的音乐——一瞥日常疲惫之外的景象。拥抱爵士神秘气质与trip-hop节奏，莱在这张抓耳的新专上迈出了值得的一步。

## 单词 Vocabulary
- **arresting**
  - 英文释义: so striking that it immediately holds your attention
  - 中文释义: 引人注目的；令人过耳不忘的
  - 例句: Embracing jazz mysticism and trip-hop rhythms, the singer-songwriter takes some worthwhile risks on her arresting new album.
  - 记忆提示: ar-rest 像“抓住”注意力，不只是“逮捕”

- **sparse instrumentation**
  - 英文释义: a thin arrangement that uses few instruments
  - 中文释义: 稀疏的配器／乐器编配
  - 例句: Past the Veil trades the intimate production and sparse instrumentation of 2021’s Geist for lush, jazzy arrangements.
  - 记忆提示: sparse≈稀少；与 lush（丰满）对照记更牢

- **cohesion**
  - 英文释义: the quality of fitting together as a united whole
  - 中文释义: 凝聚力；整体统一感
  - 例句: Lay’s voice is the rope that lassoes Past the Veil, with its menagerie of influences and references, into a sense of cohesion.
  - 记忆提示: co-（共同）+ here（粘在一起）→ 粘成一体

- **foreground**
  - 英文释义: to place someone or something in the most noticeable position
  - 中文释义: 突出；置于前景
  - 例句: Album standout “The Calling” perfectly foregrounds Lay amid a Carpenters piano melody and AM radio harmonies.
  - 记忆提示: 与 background 相对：foreground = 推到台前

- **imbue … with**
  - 英文释义: to fill something with a feeling, quality, or meaning
