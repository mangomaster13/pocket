## Vocabulary (5 items)

1. **oft-overlooked** (adjective)
   - English gloss: frequently ignored or undervalued
   - Chinese gloss: 常被忽视的
   - Example: Today, we revisit Janet’s oft-overlooked 2001 album.
   - Memory tip: oft≈often；“常被 overlook（看漏）”。

2. **thrust into the spotlight** (verb phrase)
   - English gloss: suddenly pushed into intense public attention
   - Chinese gloss: 被猛然推到聚光灯下
   - Example: At 8, she was thrust into the spotlight, making her television debut on The Carol Burnett Show.
   - Memory tip: thrust=猛推；spotlight=聚光灯→一夜成名的压迫感。

3. **demeanor** (noun)
   - English gloss: outward manner or behavior
   - Chinese gloss: 举止；神态
   - Example: The people closest to her picked up on a new demeanor; she was freer, more relaxed.
   - Memory tip: 读音像 de-MEAN-or；“你是什么 mean（意思/为人），别人从举止看出来”。

4. **awash with** (adjective phrase)
   - English gloss: full of; flooded with
   - Chinese gloss: 充斥着；满是
   - Example: The ever-competitive music industry was awash with whisperings about whether she would remain a household name.
   - Memory tip: a-wash=被水漫过→到处都是某种东西。

5. **galvanizing** (adjective)
   - English gloss: stirring people into strong excitement or action
   - Chinese gloss: 振奋人心的；激起行动的
   - Example: The tour… made it a galvanizing cultural moment.
   - Memory tip: 联想 galvanize（给金属镀锌/刺激）→“通电”般激励众人。

## Long Sentences (2-3 items)

1. **Original:** While she was preparing to enter middle age, young divas like Britney Spears and Christina Aguilera were beginning to storm the pop charts, and the ever-competitive music industry was awash with whisperings about whether she would remain a household name.
   - Structure: While-clause (time/contrast) + main clause 1 (young divas…) + and + main clause 2 (industry was awash with… + whether-clause).
   - Chinese: 正当她准备步入中年时，布兰妮、克里斯蒂娜等年轻女星开始席卷流行榜，竞争激烈的音乐圈也满是议论：她还能不能继续家喻户晓。
   - Imitation: While our team was preparing to enter a new market, younger startups were beginning to storm the rankings, and the industry was awash with whisperings about whether we would remain a household name.

2. **Original:** Pop is best served raw, when the artist becomes a vessel for emotions listeners don’t always have the language for.
   - Structure: Main claim + when-clause (condition) + relative clause (emotions [that] listeners don’t… for).
   - Chinese: 流行乐最好“生吃”——当艺人成为听众未必说得清的情绪容器时。
   - Imitation: Feedback is best served raw, when a manager becomes a vessel for concerns the team doesn’t always have the language for.

3. **Original:** Imbued with youthful wonder, the contrasting samples build a cozy bed for her daydreams of new love.
   - Structure: Past-participial opener (Imbued with…) + main clause (samples build…) + prepositional complement (for her daydreams…).
   - Chinese: 浸透着少年般的惊奇，这些对比鲜明的采样为她对新爱情的白日梦铺好一张舒适的床。
   - Imitation: Imbued with quiet confidence, the contrasting case studies build a solid base for our pitch to new clients.

## Useful Chunks (1-2 items)

- **at a turning point / at a critical point in her career** — 表示事业/人生关键节点；商务里可说 *at a critical point in the project*。
- **remain a household name** — “继续家喻户晓”；谈品牌续航时很常用。
