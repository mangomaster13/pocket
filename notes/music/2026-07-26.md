## Vocabulary (5 items)

- **call it a day** (idiom)
  - Decide to stop doing something, especially permanently or for now
  - 到此结束；收工；就此罢手
  - Ministry are calling it a day.
  - 记忆：一天干完了 → 宣布结束

- **follow through on** (phrasal verb)
  - Complete or carry out something previously promised or planned
  - 兑现；贯彻；把……做到底
  - Al Jourgensen has followed through on his long-promised announcement of a final album.
  - 记忆：follow 跟着 + through 贯穿 → 说到做到

- **farewell** (adjective)
  - Related to saying goodbye, often for the last time
  - 告别的；辞别的
  - A series of farewell shows will begin in the coming months.
  - 记忆：fare（行）+ well → 好好走，再见

- **politically charged** (adjective phrase)
  - Strongly connected to politics; likely to provoke debate
  - 带有强烈政治意味的
  - The band has also shared the video for the topical, politically charged new song “Burned Out.”
  - 记忆：charged = 带电/充满张力 → 话题很“冲”

- **at peace with** (idiom)
  - Calmly accepting a situation; no longer fighting it emotionally
  - 坦然接受；心安理得
  - I’m completely at peace with the fact that it’s done.
  - 记忆：心里平静 = 接受现实
