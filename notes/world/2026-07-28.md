# Firefighters Race to Control Wildfires Near Madrid

Source · 2026-07-28 · (no link)

## Lead
Firefighters in France and Spain are racing to contain major wildfires near Bordeaux and Madrid as another heatwave approaches. Hundreds of thousands of people have been evacuated, and officials warn that rising temperatures could make the blazes even harder to control.

### 英文改写 Readable rewrite
Firefighters across south-western France and central Spain are racing to contain vast wildfires as another heatwave builds over Europe. Near Bordeaux, crews reported a relatively calm night: several new outbreaks were put out, and the burned area did not expand. Officials say the fire is stabilised but still not under control. The blaze in the Gironde region has scorched more than 42,000 hectares, destroyed about 240 homes, and forced roughly 220,000 people to leave.

The fire has drawn within about 15 kilometres of Bordeaux’s outskirts. France’s interior minister described it as having “a life of its own,” while President Emmanuel Macron called the crisis the toughest since the Second World War and thanked responders during a visit to the region. More than 2,750 firefighters, aerial units, and around 1,500 military personnel have been mobilised. A second fire farther south in the Landes region has been contained, but the larger Gironde blaze remains the main threat.

In Spain, fires west of Madrid and in neighbouring regions have forced around 105,000 people to evacuate. Authorities say winds have pushed some flames south and away from the capital, yet villages in their path still face orders to leave. Spain’s weather service has warned of “extreme” fire risk as intense heat and dryness continue. Temperatures in Madrid and south-west France are forecast to reach the high thirties to around 39–40C, with little widespread rain expected for several days.

Residents and visitors describe thick smoke, ash, and an almost surreal atmosphere. Some Bordeaux residents compare the air to Pompeii; others are opening their homes to evacuees. Firefighters are digging and reinforcing firebreaks and hunting for smouldering embers that could reignite when easterly winds and hotter air return. Officials urge people not to go back to affected zones yet, warning that the coming days will be decisive for both countries.

在西班牙，马德里以西及邻近地区的火灾已迫使约10.5万人撤离。当局表示，风向已把部分火势推向南方、远离首都，但路径上的村庄仍面临撤离命令。西班牙气象部门警告，在持续高温与干燥条件下，火险等级为“极端”。马德里与法国西南部气温预计将升至三十多度至约39–40摄氏度，未来数日难有大范围降雨。

居民与游客描述烟雾浓重、灰烬遍地，气氛近乎不真实。一些波尔多居民把空气比作庞贝古城；另一些人则向撤离者开放自家住所。消防员正在开挖并加固防火隔离带，并搜寻可能在东风与更热气流回来时复燃的余烬。官方敦促人们暂勿返回受灾区，并警告未来几天对两国都将是关键时刻。

## 单词 Vocabulary
- **stabilised**
  - 英文释义: made steady so that a dangerous situation stops getting worse, even if it is not yet fully solved
  - 中文释义: （局势）得到稳定；暂缓恶化
  - 例句: Firefighters west of Bordeaux say the fire is stabilised – but not under control.
  - 记忆提示: stable（稳定的）+ -ise，想像“先稳住，再彻底解决”

- **evacuate**
  - 英文释义: to move people away from a dangerous place for safety
  - 中文释义: 撤离；疏散
  - 例句: An estimated 220,000 people have been forced to evacuate in the Gironde region.
  - 记忆提示: e-（向外）+ vac（空）→ 把人“清空”危险区

- **heatwave**
  - 英文释义: a period of unusually hot weather lasting several days
  - 中文释义: 热浪；持续高温期
  - 例句: Firefighters are racing to control wildfires as another heatwave hits France and Spain.
  - 记忆提示: heat（热）+ wave（浪潮）→ 像热气浪潮一样袭来

- **firebreak**
  - 英文释义: a cleared strip of land that stops or slows a wildfire’s spread
  - 中文释义: 防火隔离带
  - 例句: Overnight, crews worked to create more firebreaks by clearing brush.
  - 记忆提示: fire + break → 用空地“打断”火的蔓延

- **unprecedented**
  - 英文释义: never known or experienced before; without earlier example
  - 中文释义: 前所未有的；史无前例的
  - 例句: Macron said France is facing a completely unprecedented wildfire.
  - 记忆提示: un-（无）+ precedent（先例）→ 没有先例
