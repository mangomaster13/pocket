# Hamas Agrees to Disarm Under Trump Peace Board Plan

Source · 2026-07-31 · (no link)

## Lead
- US President Donald Trump says the Board of Peace has reached an agreement for the complete disarmament of Hamas and other armed groups in Gaza.
- A senior Hamas official told the BBC the group has accepted the plan and will issue an official statement soon, while Israel has yet to comment publicly in detail.
- Trump linked Israeli withdrawal from Gaza to completed disarmament and described the deal as a step toward a new Palestinian government.

### 英文改写 Readable rewrite

US President Donald Trump said the Board of Peace had reached an agreement for the “complete disarmament” of Hamas and other armed groups in Gaza. A senior Hamas official told the BBC that the group had agreed to the board’s plan to disarm fully in Gaza and that an official statement would be released soon. Israel had not yet issued a full public response.

Trump argued that Israel would withdraw from Gaza “as disarmament is completed” and called the deal “a critical step towards Gaza finally being governed by a new Palestinian government.” Disarmament is part of the second phase of a US-brokered ceasefire plan, which also covers reconstruction. On Truth Social, Trump called the agreement a major milestone in his 20-Point Plan and said it would move ahead in carefully structured phases. Once weapons were given up, he said, Israeli forces would pull out and an International Stabilization Force would work with a new Palestinian police force to keep Gaza safe for residents and neighbors. He thanked mediators Egypt, Qatar, and Turkey.

Egyptian state media, cited by AFP, said Cairo would host a mediator meeting on the second phase. Earlier the same day, unnamed sources told Reuters that talks in Cairo between mediators and Hamas leaders had made rare progress, though an Israeli official said the proposed terms were not satisfactory. After Trump’s announcement, the Times of Israel, citing an unnamed source, reported that negotiators from Hamas, the board, and mediating countries had signed the agreement. The same report said an Israeli office stated that the disarmament proposal did not meet Jerusalem’s demands. Israeli media had earlier said Israel opposed a deal under discussion with Hamas.

In April, Hamas rejected a disarmament plan linked to a top figure in Trump’s Gaza peace efforts, a senior Palestinian official familiar with the talks told the BBC. Earlier this month, Hamas elected Khalil al-Hayya as its new leader, replacing Yahya Sinwar, who was killed by Israeli forces in Gaza in October 2024. Hayya, previously the group’s chief negotiator, had led delegations in indirect ceasefire talks with mediators in Egypt and Qatar. The BBC reported earlier this year that the board was first thought to be focused on ending the Israel–Hamas war and overseeing reconstruction, yet its proposed charter does not mention the Palestinian territory and appears designed to take over some UN functions.

Hamas’s attack on Israel on 7 October 2023 killed about 1,200 people and saw 251 taken hostage. Israel’s military campaign in Gaza has since killed more than 73,290 people, including 21,280 children, according to the territory’s Hamas-run health ministry.

### 中文全文翻译

美国总统唐纳德·特朗普表示，“和平委员会”（Board of Peace）已就哈马斯及其他武装组织在加沙“全面解除武装”达成协议。一名哈马斯高级官员告诉BBC，该组织已同意委员会提出的在加沙彻底缴械计划，并将很快发布正式声明。以色列尚未作出完整的公开回应。

特朗普称，以色列将在“解除武装完成后”撤离加沙，并称该协议是“朝着加沙最终由新的巴勒斯坦政府治理迈出的关键一步”。解除武装属于美国斡旋的加沙停火计划第二阶段，该阶段还包括重建。特朗普在Truth Social上称这一协议是其“20点计划”落实过程中的重大里程碑，并将分阶段、审慎推进。他表示，一旦缴械完成，以军将撤离，国际稳定部队将与新建的巴勒斯坦警察部队合作，保障加沙对居民及邻国的安全。他还感谢了调解方埃及、卡塔尔和土耳其。

法新社援引埃及官方媒体报道称，开罗将就停火计划第二阶段主办调解方会议。当天早些时候，匿名消息人士对路透社称，调解方与哈马斯领导人在开罗就落实美方斡旋的和平计划举行的会谈取得少有进展，但一名以色列官员表示拟议条款并不令人满意。特朗普宣布后，《以色列时报》援引匿名消息称，哈马斯谈判代表、委员会以及调解国已签署协议。同一报道称，一个以色列机构发表声明，指解除武装方案未达到耶路撒冷的要求。以色列媒体此前还报道，以色列反对正在与哈马斯讨论的交易。

今年4月，一名熟悉谈判的巴勒斯坦高级官员告诉BBC，哈马斯曾拒绝与特朗普加沙和平努力中一位重要人物相关的解除武装计划。本月早些时候，哈马斯选举哈利勒·哈亚（Khalil al-Hayya）为新领导人，接替2024年10月在加沙被以色列军方击毙的叶海亚·辛瓦尔。哈亚此前担任该组织首席谈判代表，曾率团在埃及和卡塔尔与调解方进行间接停火谈判。BBC今年早些时候报道称，该委员会最初被认为旨在帮助结束以哈战争并监督重建，但其拟议章程并未提及巴勒斯坦领土，似乎意在取代联合国的部分职能。

2023年10月7日，哈马斯对以色列发动袭击，约1200人丧生，251人被劫为人质。以色列随后在加沙展开大规模军事行动，据加沙地带由哈马斯掌管的卫生部门称，已有超过73290人死亡，其中包括21280名儿童。

## 单词 Vocabulary
- **disarmament**
  - 英文释义: the act of giving up or removing weapons, especially by an armed group or country
  - 中文释义: 解除武装；裁军
  - 例句: Trump says the Board of Peace has reached an agreement for the complete disarmament of Hamas in Gaza.
  - 记忆提示: dis-（去掉）+ arm（武器）→ 去掉武器

- **milestone**
  - 英文释义: an important stage or turning point in a process or project
  - 中文释义: 里程碑；重大进展
  - 例句: Trump called the agreement a major milestone in the implementation of his 20-Point Plan.
  - 记忆提示: mile（英里）+ stone（石头）→ 路边里程碑，引申为关键节点

- **stabilization**
  - 英文释义: the process of making a situation more secure, calm, and under control
  - 中文释义: 稳定；维稳
  - 例句: An International Stabilization Force would work with a new Palestinian police force in Gaza.
  - 记忆提示: stable（稳定的）→ stabilize → stabilization（使…稳定的过程）

- **mediator**
  - 英文释义: a person or country that helps opposing sides reach an agreement
  - 中文释义: 调解人；斡旋方
  - 例句: Trump thanked mediators Egypt, Qatar, and Turkey for their role in the talks.
  - 记忆提示: media（中间）→ 站在中间促成和解的人

- **supplant**
  - 英文释义: to take the place of something, often by replacing its role or authority
  - 中文释义: 取代；替代
  - 例句: The board’s proposed charter appears designed to supplant functions of the UN.
  - 记忆提示: 想成“supplant = plant（植入）一个新的东西把旧的挤掉”
