## Vocabulary (5 items)
- **contain** (verb)
  - English gloss: to keep something harmful within limits; stop it spreading
  - Chinese gloss: 控制；遏制
  - Example: Firefighters are struggling to contain wildfires in south-west France.
  - Memory tip: con-（一起）+ tain（握住）→ 把火“按住不让散开”

- **evacuate** (verb)
  - English gloss: to move people out of a dangerous place for safety
  - Chinese gloss: 疏散；撤离
  - Example: In France, a further 55,000 were ordered to evacuate late on Saturday.
  - Memory tip: e-（出）+ vac（空）→ 把人撤空离开危险区

- **hamper** (verb)
  - English gloss: to make an effort slower or more difficult
  - Chinese gloss: 阻碍；妨碍
  - Example: Winds and high temperatures hamper efforts to contain them.
  - Memory tip: 像腿被绊住（hamper≈绊脚），行动变慢

- **redeploy** (verb)
  - English gloss: to move people or resources to a new place or task
  - Chinese gloss: 重新部署；调派
  - Example: Security forces can be redeployed to areas affected by wildfires.
  - Memory tip: re-（再次）+ deploy（部署）→ 再派到新任务点

- **unprecedented** (adjective)
  - English gloss: never known or experienced before
  - Chinese gloss: 前所未有的
  - Example: A situation which the government said is totally unprecedented.
  - Memory tip: un-（无）+ precedent（先例）→ 没有先例

## Long Sentences (2-3 items)
- **Original:** Firefighters are struggling to contain wildfires in south-west France as strong winds push flames towards the city of Bordeaux, with one official saying they "are far from having the fire under control."
  - Structure: [main clause] + [as-clause: simultaneous cause] + [with + noun + -ing: accompanying detail / reported speech]
  - Chinese: 消防员正艰难扑救法国西南部野火，强风把火势推向波尔多，一名官员称“远未控制住火情”。
  - Imitation: Managers are struggling to meet the deadline as client requests keep changing, with one director saying they are far from having a final plan.

- **Original:** The final leg of the Tour de France, due to take place Sunday, has been shortened from 133km (83 miles) to 89km (55 miles) so that security forces can be redeployed to areas affected by wildfires, organisers and police have said.
  - Structure: [subject + appositive “due to…”] + [passive main verb + from…to…] + [so that + purpose clause] + [reporting tag]
  - Chinese: 原定周日举行的环法最后赛段已从133公里缩短至89公里，以便安保力量可调往受野火影响地区，组织方和警方表示。
  - Imitation: The final review meeting, due to take place Friday, has been shortened from two hours to forty minutes so that staff can be redeployed to the product launch.

- **Original:** Since the wildfires broke out in Spain, nearly 60,000 people had already been evacuated from the Madrid and Ávila regions, while more than 28,000 remain confined to their homes, according to Spain's interior ministry.
  - Structure: [Since + past event] + [result in past perfect passive] + [while + contrast clause] + [according to + source]
  - Chinese: 自西班牙野火爆发以来，马德里和阿维拉地区已有近6万人撤离，另有超过2.8万人仍被困家中，西班牙内政部称。
  - Imitation: Since the system outage broke out on Monday, nearly 200 tickets had already been closed, while more than 50 remain open, according to the support team.

## Useful Chunks (1-2 items)
- **far from + -ing / noun phrase** — 强调“远未达到/完全不是”：*far from having the fire under control*
- **state of emergency** — 官方宣布的紧急状态；新闻里常与 *declare* 搭配：*a state of emergency has been declared*
