## Vocabulary (5 items)
- **hamper** (verb)
  - English gloss: to make an effort slower or more difficult
  - Chinese gloss: 妨碍；阻碍
  - Example: Winds and high temperatures hamper efforts to contain the fires.
  - Memory tip: 想成“手被绑住(hamper=篮子/束缚)就干不快”。

- **evacuate** (verb)
  - English gloss: to move people away from a dangerous place
  - Chinese gloss: 撤离；疏散
  - Example: A further 55,000 were ordered to evacuate late on Saturday.
  - Memory tip: e-(out) + vac(空) → 把人“清空出去”。

- **acrid** (adjective)
  - English gloss: sharply bitter or unpleasant, especially of smell or taste
  - Chinese gloss: 刺鼻的；辛辣刺喉的
  - Example: People reported an acrid smell in the air.
  - Memory tip: a-crid 像“刺鼻的酸”(acid)气味。

- **erratic** (adjective)
  - English gloss: irregular, unpredictable, and hard to control
  - Chinese gloss: 不规则的；难以捉摸的
  - Example: The fire was described as "erratic and unmanageable".
  - Memory tip: error-like path → 路线乱跳、不好预测。

- **unprecedented** (adjective)
  - English gloss: never known or experienced before
  - Chinese gloss: 前所未有的
  - Example: The government said the situation is totally unprecedented.
  - Memory tip: un + precedent(先例) → 没有先例。

## Long Sentences (2-3 items)
- **Original:** Firefighters are struggling to contain wildfires in south-west France as strong winds push flames towards the city of Bordeaux, with one official saying they "are far from having the fire under control."
  - Structure: main clause (Firefighters are struggling...) + reason/time clause (as strong winds push...) + absolute/with-clause adding commentary (with one official saying...)
  - Chinese: 消防员正艰难扑救法国西南部野火，强风把火势推向波尔多，一名官员称他们“远未控制住火情”。
  - Imitation: Engineers are racing to restore the network as demand spikes overnight, with one manager saying they are far from having the outage under control.

- **Original:** The final leg of the Tour de France, due to take place Sunday, has been shortened from 133km (83 miles) to 89km (55 miles) so that security forces can be redeployed to areas affected by wildfires, organisers and police have said.
  - Structure: subject + appositive (due to...) + passive main verb + result clause (so that...) + reporting tag (organisers and police have said)
  - Chinese: 组委会和警方表示，原定周日举行的环法最后赛段已从133公里缩短至89公里，以便把安保力量调往受野火影响地区。
  - Imitation: The product launch, due to take place Friday, has been delayed by two weeks so that support staff can be redeployed to critical customer issues, managers have said.

- **Original:** Since the wildfires broke out in Spain, nearly 60,000 people had already been evacuated from the Madrid and Ávila regions, while more than 28,000 remain confined to their homes, according to Spain's interior ministry.
  - Structure: since-clause (time) + main result + contrast clause (while...) + attribution (according to...)
  - Chinese: 西班牙内政部称，自野火爆发以来，马德里和阿维拉地区已有近6万人撤离，另有超过2.8万人仍被困在家中。
  - Imitation: Since the system failure began, nearly 2,000 tickets have already been closed, while more than 500 remain open, according to the operations team.

## Useful Chunks (1-2 items)
- **far from + V-ing / noun phrase** — 表示“远未……；绝非……”，语气比 not 更强：*far from having the fire under control*。
- **complex times ahead** — 政要/商务常用前景预警，指“接下来局势棘手”：admit / warn of complex times ahead。
