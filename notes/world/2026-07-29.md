# Japan Quake Kills 13 as Rescuers Race Against Time

Source · 2026-07-29 · (no link)

## Lead
A magnitude 6.8 earthquake struck Kumamoto prefecture on Japan’s Kyushu island, killing at least 13 people. Rescuers are searching collapsed sites, including a shopping mall and a factory, while the prime minister calls the effort a race against time. Aftershocks continue as thousands shelter and power and rail services remain disrupted.

### 英文改写 Readable rewrite
A powerful earthquake struck south-west Japan on Tuesday afternoon, leaving at least 13 people dead and many more searching for safety. The tremor hit Kumamoto prefecture on Kyushu island at 16:27 local time. The Japan Meteorological Agency first measured it at magnitude 7.1, while the US Geological Survey later put the figure at 6.8—a difference that is common as scientists refine early readings. The shallow epicentre, about 10 kilometres deep and roughly 20 kilometres south of Kumamoto city, helped explain why the shaking felt so severe.

Much of the urgency is focused on the Aeon Mall in Kashima, where part of the second floor collapsed and trapped shoppers and staff. The fire department said three people died there, and reporters said at least eight others had been pulled out alive. Local media first spoke of 20 to 30 people possibly trapped, then said the true number appeared lower, though officials still could not give a final count. Smoke rose from the damaged building, and large sections of its exterior were torn away, exposing steel beams. Rescue teams also worked at a paper factory in nearby Yatsushiro, where a bridge partially collapsed after the quake.

Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi offered condolences and thanked emergency crews. “It’s a race against time,” she said on Wednesday morning, warning that people still needed help more than 20 hours after the main shock. Thousands of residents moved into shelters as roads cracked, rail services stopped, and electricity failed for tens of thousands of households. Japan’s Meteorological Agency recorded more than 60 aftershocks between magnitude 1 and 4. Officials said thousands of soldiers, firefighters, and coast guard members would support the recovery.

Japan sits on one of the world’s most active seismic belts and feels about 1,500 quakes each year. Many cause little harm, but memories of the 2016 Kumamoto disasters—when hundreds died—remain vivid. On Wednesday, the country’s challenge was both technical and human: stabilise damaged sites, reach anyone still buried, and keep communities calm while the ground continues to tremble.

救援重点之一是鹿岛市的永旺购物中心：二层部分楼板坍塌，顾客和工作人员被困。消防部门称商场内有三人死亡，报道称至少另有八人被救出。当地媒体起初称可能有二三十人被困，后来表示实际人数似乎更低，但官方仍无法给出最终数字。损毁建筑冒出烟雾，外墙大面积脱落，钢梁暴露在外。附近八代市一座纸厂也在开展救援，当地一座桥梁在地震后部分坍塌。

首相高市早苗表示哀悼，并感谢应急人员。“这是一场与时间的赛跑，”她在周三上午说，并警告主震二十多小时后仍有人需要帮助。道路开裂、铁路停运、数万户断电，数千居民进入避难所。日本气象厅记录到60多次震级在1至4之间的余震。官方表示将派遣数千名军人、消防员和海岸警卫队人员支援灾后救援。

日本地处全球最活跃的地震带之一，每年约发生1500次地震。多数破坏不大，但2016年熊本地震造成数百人死亡的记忆仍历历在目。周三，日本面临的既是技术挑战，也是人道考验：稳定损毁现场，搜救可能仍被埋者，并在余震不断时安抚民众。

## 单词 Vocabulary
- **race against time**
  - 英文释义: an urgent effort to finish something before it becomes too late
  - 中文释义: 与时间赛跑；争分夺秒
  - 例句: "It's a race against time," Takaichi said in an update on Wednesday morning.
  - 记忆提示: race（赛跑）+ against time（对抗时间）= 必须尽快完成

- **death toll**
  - 英文释义: the number of people who have died in an accident, disaster, or conflict
  - 中文释义: 死亡人数
  - 例句: The death toll from a powerful earthquake in south-west Japan has risen to 13.
  - 记忆提示: toll 可指“伤亡数字”，death toll 即死亡总数

- **evacuate**
  - 英文释义: to move people from a dangerous place to a safer one
  - 中文释义: 疏散；撤离
  - 例句: The quake forced thousands to evacuate to shelters.
  - 记忆提示: e-（出）+ vac（空）→ 把人撤空离开危险区

- **aftershock**
  - 英文释义: a smaller earthquake that follows a larger one in the same area
  - 中文释义: 余震
  - 例句: Japan's Meteorological Agency has reported more than 60 aftershocks ranging in magnitude from 1 to 4.
  - 记忆提示: after（之后）+ shock（震动）→ 主震之后的震动

- **epicentre**
  - 英文释义: the point on the Earth’s surface directly above where an earthquake begins
  - 中文释义: 震中
  - 例句: The quake's shallow epicentre — with a depth of 10 km — was about 20km south of Kumamoto city.
  - 记忆提示: epi-（之上）+ centre（中心）→ 震源正上方的地表点
