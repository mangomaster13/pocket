Ol’ Dirty Bastard Has His Own New York City Street Now

Source · 2026-07-27 · 数据来源

Brooklyn has co-named a Bed-Stuy street after Wu-Tang Clan co-founder Ol’ Dirty Bastard, tying the honor to the block where his grandmother still lives. The renaming arrives more than twenty years after his death, as his family and city officials mark a lasting local legacy.

英文改写 Readable rewrite

A Brooklyn street now carries a second name that hip-hop fans will recognize at once. On July 26, Putnam Avenue in Bedford-Stuyvesant was co-named ODB Jones Way in honor of Ol’ Dirty Bastard, the Wu-Tang Clan co-founder whose raw style helped define 1990s rap. Friends, family, and local officials gathered for a ceremony that turned a neighborhood corner into a public memorial.

Although Ol’ Dirty Bastard—born Russell Tyrone Jones—grew up in Fort Greene, his ties to Bed-Stuy remain strong. At the event, his eldest brother, Ramsay Jones, told New York photographer Howie Weiss that the new sign stands at the corner of a block where their grandmother still lives. That same stretch of street is also home to a familiar mural of the cover of Jones’s first solo album, Return to the 36 Chambers: The Dirty Version. The location therefore links family history, neighborhood art, and a landmark record in one place.

The honor comes more than two decades after Jones died of an overdose in 2004, at the age of 35. In the years since, filmmakers have returned to his story more than once. An unauthorized documentary appeared in 2013, and Jones’s estate tried to stop it. A later film arrived in 2023 on A&E, this time with his family’s blessing. Together, those projects show how fiercely people still argue over how his life should be told—and how carefully his relatives guard that narrative.

Street co-naming is a quiet form of civic praise: the original avenue name stays, while a second title marks a person who shaped the city’s culture. For Bed-Stuy, ODB Jones Way is not only a tribute to a famous MC. It is also a reminder that influence can stay rooted in ordinary blocks—where a grandmother still lives, a mural still faces the sidewalk, and a new sign now asks passersby to say his name.

中文全文翻译

布鲁克林的一条街道如今有了第二个名字，嘻哈乐迷一眼就能认出来。7 月 26 日，贝德福德-史岱文森的普特南大道获加冠名“ODB Jones Way”，以纪念武当派联合创始人 Ol’ Dirty Bastard——他粗粝的风格曾深刻塑造了 1990 年代说唱。朋友、家属与地方官员齐聚仪式现场，把街角变成一处公共纪念地。

尽管 Ol’ Dirty Bastard（本名 Russell Tyrone Jones）在福特格林长大，他与 Bed-Stuy 的联系依然很深。仪式上，他的长兄 Ramsay Jones 告诉纽约摄影师 Howie Weiss，新路牌立在祖母至今仍居住的街区拐角。同一路段还有一幅广为人知的壁画，画的是他首张个人专辑《Return to the 36 Chambers: The Dirty Version》的封面。因此，这里把家族记忆、社区艺术与一张里程碑唱片连在了一起。

这一荣誉到来时，距离 Jones 2004 年因过量用药去世已逾二十年，当时他年仅 35 岁。此后，电影人多次回望他的故事。2013 年出现过一部未经授权的纪录片，Jones 的遗产管理方曾试图阻止；2023 年又有一部影片在 A&E 播出，这一次得到了家人许可。这些作品表明，人们仍在激烈争论该如何讲述他的一生，而亲属也格外谨慎地守护这一叙事权。

街道加冠名是一种低调的城市致敬：原路名保留，第二个名字则标记那位塑造城市文化的人。对 Bed-Stuy 而言，“ODB Jones Way”不仅是对一位著名 MC 的纪念，也提醒人们：影响力可以扎根在日常街区——祖母仍住在这里，壁画仍朝向人行道，而新的路牌如今让路过的人念出他的名字。

单词 Vocabulary

  • co-named

    • 英文释义: given an additional official name alongside the existing one
    • 中文释义: （街道等）被加冠名；同时拥有第二个正式名称
    • 例句: Putnam Avenue in the Bed-Stuy neighborhood was co-named ODB Jones Way.
    • 记忆提示: co- 表示“共同/附加”，name 是命名 → 额外加一个名字

  • esteemed

    • 英文释义: highly respected and admired
    • 中文释义: 备受尊敬的；声望很高的
    • 例句: A Brooklyn street has a new name courtesy of one of the city’s most esteemed rappers.
    • 记忆提示: 联想 esteem（尊重）→ esteemed 就是“被高度尊重的”

  • influential

    • 英文释义: having the power to shape ideas, taste, or other people’s work
    • 中文释义: 有影响力的
    • 例句: The street was renamed in honor of the influential group’s co-founder.
    • 记忆提示: influence（影响）+ -ial → 能产生影响的

  • unauthorized

    • 英文释义: made or released without official permission
    • 中文释义: 未经授权的
    • 例句: An unauthorized 2013 film about his life was one that Jones’ estate attempted to block.
    • 记忆提示: un-（否定）+ authorized（获准）→ 没批准就不能做

  • legacy

    • 英文释义: the lasting impact, reputation, or work someone leaves behind
    • 中文释义: 遗产；留下的深远影响
    • 例句: Two documentaries about his life and legacy have been released since his death.
    • 记忆提示: 不只是财产，也可指艺术与声誉上的“身后影响”
