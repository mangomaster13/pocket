Primark Cuts Prices Under Pressure from Shein and Temu

Source · 2026-07-27 · 数据来源

Primark is cutting prices by up to 29% on hundreds of clothing items as shoppers increasingly compare it with ultra-cheap online rivals Shein and Temu. The move comes as like-for-like sales slip and the retailer prepares for a possible stock-market spin-off. Analysts say the strategy echoes supermarket tactics: lure customers with cheap staples, then hope they fill their baskets with more.

英文改写 Readable rewrite

Primark, the fast-fashion chain with more than 190 stores in the UK, said on Monday that it would cut prices by up to 29% across hundreds of items, including jeans, jumpers and socks. Shoppers have been telling reporters that Primark no longer feels as cheap as it once did. A 19-year-old student browsing jewellery in store said the brand had "definitely got more expensive," and many younger buyers now measure its prices against Shein dresses that can sell for as little as £3.

Retail analyst Natalie Berg called the announcement surprising for such a low-cost retailer. Price cuts of this kind are the sort of headline people expect from mid-market names such as Marks & Spencer or Next, she said. Joining a "race to the bottom" is risky, but when Chinese online marketplaces set a new baseline for "cheap," Primark has little choice but to respond. Competition has also widened to TikTok Shop and second-hand platforms such as Vinted.

Higher fabric prices, rising minimum wages, shipping and energy costs, and a push for higher standards have all helped push clothing prices up. Primark has also seen a drop in like-for-like sales, a key retail measure. The cuts come ahead of parent company Associated British Foods' plan to spin the business off onto the London stock market next year. By cutting prices on core staples, Primark hopes to win shoppers back the way supermarkets use cheap milk and bananas to pull people through the door.

Shein and Temu can undercut High Street chains partly because they avoid physical-store costs, benefit from import-duty exemptions due to end in October 2028, and run lean models with little unsold stock. Primark offers click-and-collect but not home delivery, arguing that this keeps operations simple and savings with customers. About 85% of its products already cost £10 or less, and the company says the new lower prices are permanent rather than a short promotion.

Analysts still see the campaign as partly a marketing exercise: shoppers drawn in by cheaper jeans often leave with other impulse buys. Sharper prices should also help Primark clear stock without deeper later discounts. In a tough cost-of-living climate, where roughly a third of clothing shoppers say they buy less often, Berg's conclusion is blunt: if you claim to be the UK's value fashion retailer, you have to deliver on that promise.

中文全文翻译

拥有逾190家英国门店的快时尚品牌Primark周一表示，将把包括牛仔裤、毛衣和袜子在内的数百种商品价格下调最高29%。消费者近来常对记者说，Primark已不再像从前那样便宜。一名在店内挑选首饰的19岁学生称，这个品牌“明显变贵了”；许多年轻买家如今会拿它与Shein上低至3英镑的连衣裙比价。

零售分析师Natalie Berg认为，对这样一家定位低价的零售商来说，此次降价声明令人意外。这类价格新闻人们更常期待出现在Marks & Spencer或Next等中端品牌身上。她指出，卷入“逐底竞争”风险很大，但当中国线上平台重新定义了“便宜”的基准，Primark几乎别无选择，只能回应。竞争阵营还扩大到TikTok Shop以及Vinted等二手平台。

面料涨价、最低工资上调、运费与能源成本上升，以及对更高标准的追求，共同推高了服装价格。Primark的同店销售额——零售业一项关键指标——也出现下滑。此次降价恰逢母公司Associated British Foods计划明年将业务分拆并在伦敦上市。通过下调核心基础款价格，Primark希望像超市用廉价牛奶和香蕉引流一样，把顾客重新吸引进店。

Shein和Temu之所以能压过高街品牌，部分原因在于它们无需承担实体店成本、享受将于2028年10月结束的进口关税豁免，并以库存积压很少的精益模式运营。Primark提供线上下单门店自取，但不提供送货上门，理由是这样能简化运营并把节省下来的成本留给顾客。约85%的商品售价已在10英镑或以下；公司称，新的更低价格是长期定价，而非短期促销。

分析人士仍认为，此次举措带有营销成分：被更便宜牛仔裤吸引进店的顾客，往往会顺带冲动购买其他商品。更锋利的价格也有助于Primark去库存，避免日后更深折扣。在艰难的生活成本环境下，约三分之一服装消费者表示购买频率下降；Berg的结论很直接：如果你自称是英国的价值时尚零售商，就必须兑现这一承诺。

单词 Vocabulary

  • like-for-like sales

    • 英文释义: sales compared with the same stores over the same period, used to judge true trading performance
    • 中文释义: 同店销售额；在可比门店、可比时段内衡量真实经营表现的销售指标
    • 例句: Primark has been experiencing a drop in like-for-like sales, a key measure in the retail industry.
    • 记忆提示: like-for-like＝“苹果对苹果”的可比；看增长时先问是不是同店口径

  • race to the bottom

    • 英文释义: a harmful competition in which rivals keep cutting prices or standards to win customers
    • 中文释义: 逐底竞争；各方不断压低价格或标准以争夺客户的恶性竞争
    • 例句: "You don't want to join a race to the bottom," she says. "But when Shein is selling dresses for £3, you've got to respond, right?"
    • 记忆提示: race＝赛跑，bottom＝底部；大家一起往价格谷底冲

  • spin off

    • 英文释义: to separate part of a company into an independent business, often for listing on a stock market
    • 中文释义: 分拆；把公司一部分独立出来，常为上市做准备
    • 例句: The announcement of price cuts comes ahead of parent company Associated British Foods' plan to spin off the business onto the London stock market next year.
    • 记忆提示: spin＝旋转甩出；把子公司“甩”出去独立运营

  • undercut

    • 英文释义: to offer lower prices than competitors in order to win their customers
    • 中文释义: 削价竞争；以更低价格抢夺对手客户
    • 例句: Temu and Shein have been able to undercut traditional retailers like Primark because they do not pay for physical locations.
    • 记忆提示: under＝在下面，cut＝砍；把价格砍到对手下面

  • impulse buy

    • 英文释义: an unplanned purchase made on the spur of the moment, often after entering a store for something else
    • 中文释义: 冲动消费；因一时兴起、非事先计划的购买
    • 例句: Even if shoppers are drawn in by cheaper jeans, they will probably end up putting other impulse items in their basket too.
    • 记忆提示: impulse＝冲动；被低价引流进店后“顺手多买一件”

长难句

  • 原句： By slashing prices on some core items, Primark is likely hoping to woo shoppers with low-cost staples, in the same way that supermarkets attract customers with cheap milk and bananas.

    • 结构: By doing...（方式状语）+ 主句 + in the same way that...（方式类比从句）
    • 中文: Primark通过大幅下调部分核心单品价格，很可能是希望用低价基础款吸引顾客，就像超市用廉价牛奶和香蕉引流一样。
    • 仿写: By offering free basic shipping, the platform is likely hoping to woo first-time buyers with low friction, in the same way that banks attract clients with fee-free starter accounts.

  • 原句： Temu and Shein have been able to undercut traditional retailers like Primark because they do not pay for physical locations, most of their packages are exempt from import duties – though this exemption is set to end in October 2028 – and they have an efficient operating model that leaves them with little unsold stock.

    • 结构: 主句 + because引导三个并列原因（无实体店成本 / 关税豁免 / 精益库存），中间插入让步说明豁免将到期
    • 中文: Temu和Shein之所以能压过Primark这类传统零售商，是因为它们无需支付实体店成本、多数包裹享受进口关税豁免——尽管该豁免将于2028年10月结束——并且拥有库存积压很少的高效运营模式。
    • 仿写: Online grocers have been able to undercut neighbourhood shops because they centralise warehousing, many deliveries avoid peak-hour labour costs – though new city rules may change that – and they use demand data that leaves them with little wasted stock.

  • 原句： Getting a couple of pounds off a jumper or a pair of jeans might not seem like a lot to a customer, but for a shop with tight margins like Primark, how might this affect the business?

    • 结构: 让步对比句：对顾客似乎不大（might not seem...）+ but转折 + 对低利润商家的影响设问
    • 中文: 毛衣或牛仔裤便宜几英镑，对顾客来说或许不算什么；但对Primark这样利润很薄的商店而言，这会如何影响生意呢？
    • 仿写: Shaving a few dollars off a software subscription might not seem like a lot to a user, but for a start-up with tight margins, how might this affect cash flow?
