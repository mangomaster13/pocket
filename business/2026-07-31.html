Three things we learned about AI from Big Tech earnings

Source · 2026-07-31 · 数据来源

Big Tech firms told Wall Street they will keep pouring money into AI, even as investors demand clearer proof of returns. Meta and Alphabet reported very weak free cash flow, while Microsoft and Amazon drew a warmer market response. Apple said strong demand for Macs, iPhones and iPads is running into chip shortages, and it sees paid potential in a rebuilt Siri.

英文改写 Readable rewrite

This week, Microsoft, Meta, Google, Apple and Amazon updated Wall Street on their latest finances. Across the reports, one theme stood out: all of them plan to keep spending huge sums on artificial intelligence. Investors, however, were less patient. After more than $1 trillion has already gone into chips, data centres and technical staff, many wanted clearer, more tangible results. That tension sent several tech stocks on a sharp ride in recent days.

Consumer chatbots are everywhere now. Meta has Meta AI, Google has Gemini, Amazon has Rufus, and Apple has relaunched Siri. Yet these tools still do not clearly generate meaningful revenue on their own, even though they are expensive to build. Alphabet and Meta made that gap especially obvious. Alphabet reported negative free cash flow on $118 billion of revenue after heavy AI spending—the first time as a public company that it spent more than it brought in. Meta’s free cash flow was only $784 million on $61 billion of revenue, and Reality Labs, which handles much of its AI work, lost nearly $9 billion in the first half of the year.

Wall Street’s reaction to Meta showed how little comfort vague future promises now buy. Shares fell to their second-lowest level in a year after Mark Zuckerberg described plans for an AI agent and a business selling AI tools to other firms—ideas that do not yet exist in a money-making form, and that came with no clear timeline. Meta still raised the low end of its AI spending plans and may pour more than $140 billion into AI this year.

Microsoft told a different story. Even with plans to match the roughly $190 billion spent on AI over the past year, it reported strong revenue growth and wider adoption of its core AI tools. Shares rose to a six-month high, and analyst Tracy Woo said the investments were “beginning to deliver returns.” Amazon faced a similar market mood: despite negative cash flow and plans to spend $220 billion on AI this year, strength in its other businesses pushed the stock to a two-month high.

Demand for new technology remains large even if AI has not yet become a consumer revolution on the scale of the internet. Google said 950 million people use Gemini at least once a month—three times last year’s figure. Apple said Macs, iPhones and iPads sold better than expected, but warned that sales may slow because it cannot secure enough microchips. The company is still counting on excitement around an upgraded Siri, built with help from Google’s Gemini, and Tim Cook said Apple already plans to charge heavy users. “We’re off-the-charts excited about Siri AI,” he said.

中文全文翻译

本周，微软、Meta、谷歌、苹果和亚马逊向华尔街更新了最新财务情况。各份财报中有一个突出主题：它们都计划继续在人工智能上投入巨额资金。不过，投资者已不那么有耐心。在芯片、数据中心和技术人员上已投入逾一万亿美元之后，许多人希望看到更清晰、更切实的成果。这种张力让部分科技股近日大幅波动。

面向消费者的聊天机器人如今随处可见：Meta 有 Meta AI，谷歌有 Gemini，亚马逊有 Rufus，苹果也重新推出了 Siri。但这些工具本身仍未能明显带来可观收入，尽管开发成本很高。Alphabet 和 Meta 把这一落差暴露得尤其清楚。在大规模 AI 支出后，Alphabet 在 1180 亿美元营收下录得负自由现金流——这是其作为上市公司首次入不敷出。Meta 在 610 亿美元营收下自由现金流仅 7.84 亿美元；负责大量 AI 业务的 Reality Labs 上半年亏损近 90 亿美元。

华尔街对 Meta 的反应表明，含糊的未来承诺如今已难安抚投资者。马克·扎克伯格谈到计划打造可一定程度自主运行的 AI 智能体，以及向其他公司销售 AI 工具的业务后，股价跌至一年内第二低；这些设想目前尚无法真正赚钱，也没有明确时间表。即便如此，Meta 仍上调了 AI 支出计划下限，今年可能向 AI 投入逾 1400 亿美元。

微软的情况不同。尽管计划在本财年继续匹配过去约 1900 亿美元的 AI 支出规模，它仍报告了强劲营收增长和核心 AI 工具更广泛的采用。股价升至六个月高位，分析师 Tracy Woo 称这些投资“开始带来回报”。亚马逊面对的市场情绪类似：尽管现金流为负，且计划今年在 AI 上花费 2200 亿美元，其他业务的强劲表现仍把股价推至两个月高点。

即便 AI 尚未成为互联网级别的消费科技革命，人们对新技术的需求依然巨大。谷歌表示，每月至少使用一次 Gemini 的用户达 9.5 亿，是一年前的三倍。苹果称 Mac、iPhone 和 iPad 卖得比预期更好，但警告因无法获得足够微芯片，销售可能放缓。公司仍寄望借助谷歌 Gemini 升级的 Siri 激发用户热情，蒂姆·库克表示苹果已计划向重度用户收费。他说：“我们对 Siri AI 兴奋到爆表。”

单词 Vocabulary

  • tangible results

    • 英文释义: outcomes that can be clearly seen, measured, or proven
    • 中文释义: 看得见、可衡量的实际成果
    • 例句: Investors need to see more tangible results for the trillion-dollar AI investment.
    • 记忆提示: tangible 原指“可触摸的”，引申为“实实在在的”

  • free cash flow

    • 英文释义: money left after a company pays for operations and investments
    • 中文释义: 自由现金流；支付经营与投资后剩余的现金
    • 例句: Alphabet’s free cash flow was negative on revenue of $118bn.
    • 记忆提示: free = 可自由支配；看公司“手头还剩多少真金白银”

  • plunged

    • 英文释义: fell suddenly and sharply
    • 中文释义: （价格等）骤跌、暴跌
    • 例句: Shares of the social media giant plunged to its second lowest level in a year.
    • 记忆提示: 像“跳水”一样迅速往下掉

  • placated

    • 英文释义: calmed or satisfied so that anger or doubt is reduced
    • 中文释义: 被安抚；使平静、平息不满
    • 例句: Investors are no longer placated with claims that AI will pay off someday.
    • 记忆提示: 想成“用承诺把人哄住”，但现在已经哄不住了

  • impending

    • 英文释义: about to happen soon
    • 中文释义: 即将到来的；迫近的
    • 例句: Apple is anticipating excitement for its impending update of Siri.
    • 记忆提示: im-（向内）+ pend（悬挂）→ 悬在眼前、马上发生

长难句

  • 原句： The reaction from investors was that they need to see more tangible results to show for the $1tn and growing investment in things like computer chips, data centres, and even technical staff.

    • 结构: 主句 The reaction was that… + 表语从句；to show for… 说明“这笔投入应对应得见的回报”
    • 中文: 投资者的反应是：对于已达一万亿美元且仍在增加的芯片、数据中心乃至技术人员投入，他们需要看到更多切实成果来证明这笔钱花得值。
    • 仿写: The board’s concern was that managers need to show clearer productivity gains to justify the rising spend on automation tools and training.

  • 原句： Google spent so much money on AI that Alphabet’s free cash flow was negative on revenue of $118bn, meaning it spent more than it brought in for the first time in the company’s history as a public company.

    • 结构: so… that… 结果状语；meaning… 分词短语补充说明含义
    • 中文: 谷歌在 AI 上花费如此之巨，以致 Alphabet 在 1180 亿美元营收下自由现金流为负，这意味着它作为上市公司首次入不敷出。
    • 仿写: The startup hired so aggressively that its cash runway shrank to six months, meaning it spent faster than it raised for the first time since launch.

  • 原句： Despite the company’s plans to effectively match this financial year the $190bn it spent on AI over the last 12 months, it showed strong revenue growth and more adoption of its core AI tool.

    • 结构: Despite + 名词短语作让步；主句 it showed… 给出与压力相反的积极结果
    • 中文: 尽管该公司计划在本财年基本匹配过去 12 个月约 1900 亿美元的 AI 支出，它仍展现出强劲的营收增长和核心 AI 工具更广泛的采用。
    • 仿写: Despite the firm’s plan to keep capital spending near last year’s record level, it posted faster sales growth and wider use of its cloud platform.
