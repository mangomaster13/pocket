Carrie Seeks Friends in Flanagan’s Prime Video Teaser

Source · 2026-07-26 · 数据来源

Prime Video has released the first teaser for Mike Flanagan’s new limited series *Carrie*, adapted from Stephen King’s classic. The eight-episode show premieres on October 7 and follows lonely teen Carrie White as bullying, social media pressure, and telekinetic powers collide. Flanagan writes, executive produces, and directs four episodes, with Stephen King also serving as an executive producer.

英文改写 Readable rewrite

A lonely Carrie White searches for friendship and answers in the first teaser for Carrie, a new adaptation of Stephen King’s classic story from horror filmmaker Mike Flanagan. Alongside the teaser, Prime Video announced that the eight-episode limited series will premiere on October 7.

The series follows misfit high-schooler Carrie White, played by Summer H. Howell. For much of her life she has been kept inside her home by her fiercely protective mother, Margaret, played by Samantha Sloyan. After her father’s sudden death forces her into the harsh world of public high school, Carrie must face a viral bullying scandal that spreads through her community. She also confronts the casual cruelty of the social-media age and the awakening of mysterious telekinetic powers that grow with her adolescence.

Mike Flanagan, known for Midnight Mass and The Haunting of Hill House, serves as showrunner. He writes and executive produces the series and directs four episodes. Stephen King is also an executive producer. The cast includes Siena Agudong as Sue Snell, Alison Thornton as Chris Hargensen, Joel Oulette as Tommy Ross, Amber Midthunder as Miss Desjardin, and Matthew Lillard as Principal Grayle.

Each episode is expected to open with a different story about another woman, somewhere else in the world and in time, coming to terms with her own abilities. Flanagan has said that Carrie’s place among that group of women is part of the joy viewers will discover across the season. He appears ready to expand King’s story in unexpected ways through a modern lens, and a new poster offers a glimpse of Carrie in her prom queen tiara.

单词 Vocabulary

  • misfit

    • 英文释义: a person who does not fit in with a group or social setting
    • 中文释义: 不合群的人；格格不入者
    • 例句: The series follows misfit high-schooler Carrie White.
    • 记忆提示: mis-（错）+ fit（适合）→ 合不进来的人

  • fiercely protective

    • 英文释义: extremely determined to shield someone from harm or outside influence
    • 中文释义: 极力保护的；过度护短的
    • 例句: Carrie has spent her life hidden away with her fiercely protective mother.
    • 记忆提示: fiercely 强调“猛烈、极度”，protective 是保护 → 保护欲极强

  • viral bullying scandal

    • 英文释义: a case of harassment that spreads quickly online and becomes a public controversy
    • 中文释义: 迅速在网上传播的霸凌丑闻
    • 例句: Carrie is forced to navigate a viral bullying scandal that tears through her community.
    • 记忆提示: viral 像病毒一样传播；scandal 是丑闻

  • telekinetic powers

    • 英文释义: the ability to move objects with the mind
    • 中文释义: 念力；心灵致动能力
    • 例句: Mysterious telekinetic powers rise alongside her adolescence.
    • 记忆提示: tele-（远距离）+ kinetic（运动）→ 用意念让物体动起来

  • limited series

    • 英文释义: a TV show planned for a fixed, short number of episodes rather than many seasons
    • 中文释义: 限时剧集；短篇季播剧
    • 例句: The eight-episode limited series premieres on Prime Video on October 7.
    • 记忆提示: limited 表示“有限”，series 是剧集 → 集数固定、通常一季完结

  • 原句： Expect Flanagan to expand King’s story in unexpected ways, using a modern lens.
