Firefighters battle wildfire as tens of thousands evacuated near Bordeaux

Source · 2026-07-27 · 数据来源

Firefighters are racing to contain huge wildfires near Bordeaux after tens of thousands of people fled villages in southwest France overnight. Across France and Spain, more than 330,000 people have been evacuated as heat, dry vegetation, and strong winds push the blazes further. Officials say Bordeaux itself is unlikely to be evacuated for now, but the fire is still moving erratically toward the city.

英文改写 Readable rewrite

Firefighters are fighting to contain wildfires burning near Bordeaux after tens of thousands of people were ordered out of nearby villages. France’s Interior Minister Laurent Nuñez said the situation remained “very unfavourable,” warning that the fire was “moving erratically towards” the city. Officials still played down the chance that Bordeaux itself would need to be evacuated.

Overnight into Sunday, about 55,000 people left villages south of the city in Gironde, a region famous for its vineyards. In total, more than 220,000 people have been evacuated across southwest France. About 42,000 hectares have burned there, making the blaze one of the largest forest fires in the country since World War Two. Across Europe over the past week, wildfires—mainly in France and Spain—have forced more than 330,000 people from their homes. About 98,000 hectares have burned in France since the start of the year.

Captain Nicolas Braz, one of the operation’s leaders, said the fire had created “its own winds—complete with whirlwinds” and called it “erratic and unmanageable.” In the coastal town of Le Porge, more than 150 houses have been destroyed. Local volunteers have cooked meals and offered rest spaces for exhausted firefighters. “I think many of them have never experienced a fire of this magnitude before,” volunteer Nathalie Augonnet said.

In Spain, more than 90,000 people have been evacuated from areas around Madrid and Ávila, where Ecological Transition Minister Sara Aagesen said about 77,000 hectares had been affected and called the Ávila fire the largest in the country’s recent history. Near Valencia, regional leaders warned that a new fire front was beyond their capacity to extinguish, with high temperatures and low humidity expected to worsen conditions. One civilian died in a smaller Valencia blaze; earlier in the week, two firefighters were killed near Bordeaux.

In Gironde, about 2,500 firefighters—including a Swiss team—plus 1,500 military personnel and nearly 1,200 police and gendarmes are supporting rescue and security work. Dry vegetation and brisk winds have helped the fire spread, and a long stretch of the A63 motorway toward Spain has been closed. Bordeaux Airport delayed commercial flights under air-traffic restrictions meant to free space for emergency services, and the Tour de France’s final stage was shortened so security forces could reinforce the firefighting effort. President Emmanuel Macron said France would “rebuild” and stand with affected communities “for as long as is needed.”

中文全文翻译

消防人员正努力控制波尔多附近燃烧的野火；此前，周边村庄已有数万人接到撤离命令。法国内政部长洛朗·努涅斯表示，当地局势仍“非常不利”，并警告火势正“不规则地向”波尔多推进。不过，官方仍淡化了波尔多市区本身需要撤离的可能性。

进入周日的一夜之间，波尔多以南吉伦特省约5.5万人离开村庄；该地区以葡萄园闻名。法国西南部累计已有超过22万人撤离。当地约4.2万公顷土地被烧毁，使这场大火成为二战以来该国最大的森林火灾之一。过去一周，欧洲野火——主要发生在法国和西班牙——已迫使超过33万人离开家园。自今年年初以来，法国已有约9.8万公顷土地被烧毁。

灭火行动负责人之一尼古拉·布拉兹队长表示，大火已形成“自己的风——还带着旋风”，并称火势“反复无常、难以控制”。在沿海小镇勒波尔热，已有逾150栋房屋被毁。当地志愿者为疲惫的消防员做饭，并提供休息场所。志愿者娜塔莉·奥戈内说：“我觉得他们中很多人从未经历过如此规模的火灾。”

在西班牙，马德里与阿维拉周边地区已有逾9万人撤离。生态转型部长萨拉·阿赫森表示，西班牙约7.7万公顷土地受影响，并称阿维拉大火是该国近年史上最大的一场。巴伦西亚附近，地方官员警告新火线已超出其扑灭能力，高温与低湿度预计会让周日形势进一步恶化。巴伦西亚一场较小火灾造成一名平民死亡；本周早些时候，两名消防员在波尔多附近灭火时牺牲。

在吉伦特省，约2500名消防员（含一支瑞士队伍）、1500名军人和近1200名警察与宪兵正在协助救援与安保。干燥植被与疾风助长火势蔓延，通往西班牙的A63高速路有很长一段关闭。波尔多机场因空管限制延误商业航班，以便为应急服务腾出空间；环法自行车赛最终赛段也被缩短，以便安保力量增援灭火。总统埃马纽埃尔·马克龙表示，法国将“重建”，并将“需要多久就支持多久”。

单词 Vocabulary

  • evacuate

    • 英文释义: to move people away from a dangerous place to somewhere safer
    • 中文释义: 撤离；疏散
    • 例句: Overnight into Sunday, about 55,000 people were evacuated from villages south of Bordeaux.
    • 记忆提示: e-（向外）+ vac（空）→ 把人“清空”到安全处

  • erratically

    • 英文释义: in an irregular, unpredictable way
    • 中文释义: 不规则地；反复无常地
    • 例句: The interior minister said the fire was moving erratically towards Bordeaux.
    • 记忆提示: 联想 error（出错）→ 路线“出错般”乱窜

  • unfavourable

    • 英文释义: not helpful or encouraging; likely to make a situation worse
    • 中文释义: 不利的；不乐观的
    • 例句: Laurent Nuñez said the situation in the region remained “very unfavourable.”
    • 记忆提示: un-（否定）+ favourable（有利的）→ 不利

  • magnitude

    • 英文释义: the great size or seriousness of something
    • 中文释义: 规模；严重程度
    • 例句: Many firefighters have never experienced a fire of this magnitude before.
    • 记忆提示: 地震里常说 magnitude（震级）→ 这里指大火的“量级”

  • redeploy

    • 英文释义: to move people or resources to a new place or task
    • 中文释义: 重新部署；调配
    • 例句: The Tour de France stage was shortened to allow security forces to be redeployed to the firefighting effort.
    • 记忆提示: re-（再次）+ deploy（部署）→ 再次调过去支援

长难句

  • 原句： France's Interior Minister Laurent Nuñez said the situation in the region remained "very unfavourable" and the fire was "moving erratically towards" Bordeaux, though officials downplayed the possibility of evacuation.

    • 结构: 主句（部长 said + 两个并列宾语从句）+ though 引导的让步状语从句
    • 中文: 法国内政部长洛朗·努涅斯表示，该地区局势仍“非常不利”，火势正“不规则地向”波尔多推进，不过官方淡化了撤离的可能性。
    • 仿写: The city’s mayor said traffic remained “very heavy” and the storm was “moving slowly towards” downtown, though officials downplayed the possibility of a full shutdown.

  • 原句： Rescue efforts in France's Gironde region involve 2,500 firefighters, including a Swiss team, 1,500 military personnel and almost 1,200 police and gendarmes, who are carrying out evacuations and helping with rescue and securing operations.

    • 结构: 主句列出多方力量 + including 插入补充 + who 非限制性定语从句说明职责
    • 中文: 法国吉伦特省的救援行动涉及2500名消防员（含一支瑞士队伍）、1500名军人以及近1200名警察与宪兵，他们正在执行撤离并协助救援与安保行动。
    • 仿写: The relief plan involves 800 doctors, including a volunteer unit, 200 engineers and almost 500 drivers, who are delivering supplies and helping with shelter and medical care.

  • 原句： The final stage of the Tour de France was shortened on Sunday from 133km to 89km (from 82 miles to 55) to allow security forces to be redeployed to the firefighting effort.

    • 结构: 被动主句（赛段被缩短）+ from...to... 数量变化 + to allow... 目的状语
    • 中文: 周日，环法自行车赛最终赛段从133公里缩短至89公里（82英里至55英里），以便将安保力量重新部署到灭火行动中。
    • 仿写: The marathon route was shortened on Saturday from 42km to 30km to allow medical teams to be redeployed to the emergency clinics.
